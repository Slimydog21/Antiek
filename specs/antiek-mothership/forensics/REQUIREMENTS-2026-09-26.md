## R18–R40 (addendum, 2026-09-26)
- **R18** Spin up a sub-agent by highlighting a sub-agent's output and re-prompting it, as with highlights of primary evidence.
- **R19** Such a sub-sub-agent hardens an insight or chases an open question from the parent's output; several lanes of thought are held at once.
- **R20** Merge sub-agent analyses together manually, with a draft shown before the merge is accepted.
- **R21** Merged threads group ideas for the agent-facing data landscape and enrich a growing sub-agent's context; sub-agents can be called across projects.
- **R22** Re-imagine and redesign the home page of every product, with a smooth transition into the mothership (cockpit) workflow.
- **R23** Jump between products and into motherships by key commands.
- **R24** A key hierarchy: project-level keys launch into a familiar mothership; mothership-level keys move between documents in the left pane and the sub-agents attached to them on the right.
- **R25** Agent-first single pane: discuss with one agent or a group of agents in a single pane the size of a cockpit pane (effectively a right pane); it may open primary reading on the left when needed; the fullscreen key expands it (Omarchy).
- **R26** An add-new-project key opens a home-like create-a-project page.
- **R27** Starting a new project means something different in Research, Writing, Reading (Books), Speaking and Autonomous Research.
- **R28** Transfers between products, whole or partial: Research → Writing (direct); Speak → Writing; Research → Autonomous Research (sub-agent-level management); Research → Books (choose long-form asset(s) to deep read).
- **R29** Sub-agents can grow beyond projects: sub-agents working across several projects.
- **R30** Casual-interest reading projects (e.g. "Airplanes"): sub-agents on the right, a series of reading assets on the left.
- **R31** Rebrand the Reading product to **Books**: the core is spending time with a book's ideas, strictly book-focused, recovering the warmth of reading; a sub-agent may temporarily open another asset on the left down a rabbit hole.
- **R32** Books has an autonomous notebook: the sum of the learnings and insights of the sub-agents across the book and its context.
- **R33** Books may select a Research project, while staying strict about the book focus.
- **R34** Voice as an input for prompting across all products.
- **R35** A new product, **Converse**: select a project (Research, Writing, a Book, …) and talk to it.
- **R36** Converse monologues, audiobook-like and time-bound: "I have the next 30 minutes free, explain X to me in that time."
- **R37** In Converse, prompt by text while the conversation unfolds, and by voice notes.
- **R38** Speak's objective: gather insights through frictionless voice notes from private connections and the general public.
- **R39** Speak manages my requests by project, my private inbound invites, and navigation of public requests.
- **R40** Speak's layout: three sections on the left pane (My projects, Invites, Public); selecting one gives its detail layer in the right pane.
