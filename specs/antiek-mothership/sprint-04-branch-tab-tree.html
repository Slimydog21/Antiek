What this sprint is about. The operator (D6, 2026-09-24): sub-branches from sub-branches should be "infinitely layered to represent the precision of the logic tree". Opening a footnote, reference, citation, island or deep research from a tab spawns a child tab. There is no tab concept on main at all (SW-03, N4). The only unbounded tree on main is the investigation tree (parent_investigation_id → the recursive InvestigationSidebar), which is the model to follow.

Tab ≠ branch (agreed with lane B). The tab tree is navigation state, server-side per account through the single-writer API. The research branch is provenance, written to the parent investigation's log before the child starts. Pruning a tab never deletes a branch. The design is in DESIGN-MODEL.md §2a:

  • a path header, compressed in the middle past about four crumbs
  • a sibling strip with a ↳ n child chip
  • a tree panel with a six-level indent cap, a depth badge and subtree focus
  • hierarchical numbers used as addresses (3.2.1)
  • keys prefix+u/o/n/p/t
  • prune by default, or close-only-this, with 10 s undo

This is the design's single signature element. Spend boldness here and keep everything around it quiet.

Base: MS-03. Branch: design/mothership-04-branch-tree.

Contract facts (THREAD-CONTRACT.md rev 7, signed by both lanes 2026-09-26): these override any older wording on this page.
  • Tab persistence is GET /projects/{id}/tabs/{mothership} → {tree, active_tab_id, version} and PUT with {tree, active_tab_id, expected_version} (§1.6). Write debounced whole-tree snapshots. On 409 {current}, replay pending local operations on current and PUT again; an operation on a tab another device closed is dropped with one quiet toast (Part 2 §2.2).
  • public_number comes from POST /projects/{id}/tabs/{mothership}/allocate: workstation-wide, never reused even after close. Until it returns, the tab is in numbering (open, reachable by n/p/w, not by digit). Never invent a number locally.
  • hier_number is computed client-side as <parent>.<next unused child index> counting retired numbers, validated on the PUT and final once accepted; on a 409, recompute pending spawns before acceptance so every local spawn survives (R3-5). Closed and pruned numbers go to retired_numbers keyed by tab_id; restoring the same tab_id reclaims them, a different one never may (R3-1). Hold a close locally through its 10 s undo window so other devices never see an undone close.
  • A branch is a durable dependency, not proof the child ran (§1.3). A thread tab can read no_record (a crash-orphan or a lost log: the branch stays live and fails closed), abandoned (only capacity_refused or an operator reconciliation) or spawn_refused (422 parent_investigation_not_found or 409 reservation_parent_mismatch); each is designed (Part 2 §2.2). Tabs never point at reserved-only ids. A pending spawn whose parent closed remotely re-attaches to the nearest surviving ancestor (or root) with a fresh number; a replayed prune never closes tabs this device hasn't seen (lift them). Restore from history reads the server's retired[] descriptors. branch_origin.kind "island" is selection in the backend.
Where this sprint sits: Wave 1, after MS-03. MS-05 (a document opened from a citation becomes a child tab) and MS-06 (Reading's rabbit holes) depend on it.

Goal

Every mothership holds a forest of branch tabs per workstation. Any depth is reachable in two keystrokes by number, and the tree stays legible and fast at the operator's real scale.

The fixture is his herdr scale: 20 workstations, one with 174 tabs, and depth greater than 12 in at least one branch. A tab records its parent, its branch origin (the passage it was opened from) and its stable hierarchical number. The UI makes the tree the thing you steer by: the path to where you are, your siblings, and the whole tree on demand.

Numbered. Each milestone has explicit acceptance criteria and the files it touches. Do not declare a milestone complete unless the criteria are mechanically verified.

1
Tab tree model (contract §2.2), local-first

A tab-tree store: {tab_id, parent_tab_id, branch_origin{doc_id,locator,kind}, hier_number, child_order, last_visited_child_id, pruned_at, kind, ref, mothership}. Numbers are stable, never reused under the same parent, and never renumbered. It persists through an adapter: an in-memory implementation now, and lane B's tab-tree API (§1.6) once W1 ships, following the same adapter pattern as MS-03. Tab state never goes to localStorage or sessionStorage (§1.6).

Acceptance criteria
  • Property tests: numbers stay unique and stable under random spawn, prune, lift and undo sequences (≥ 1,000 generated sequences).
  • Depth is unbounded (a test at depth 200).

6
Numbers as addresses + tree keys

The switcher (MS-03) resolves 3.2.1 to a tab. prefix+1..9 reaches root tabs, prefix+u the parent (returning to the exact origin locator), prefix+o the last-visited child, and prefix+n/p the next/previous sibling. The chord twins come from MS-01's table. ctrl+alt+t is Ubuntu/Fedora's launch-terminal key, so give tree-panel the chord ctrl+alt+y, recording why in the table.

Acceptance criteria
  • Keyboard tests for every tree key, including focus return and the exact-locator return on prefix+u.
apps/reading/src/components/hotkeys/keymap.ts
7
Prune / close-only-this with undo

Close offers prune (the default: the tab and its subtree) or close-only-this (children lift to the parent). Both can be undone for 10 s. On main, LemonToast has no action slot (its lifetimes are 4/6/8 s), so this milestone adds an undo action with a 10 000 ms lifetime. If W2 added a toast action first, reuse it. Pruned subtrees stay in history; branches are never deleted.

Acceptance criteria
  • Tests: prune+undo restores numbers exactly, lift keeps children's numbers stable, and a pruned research tab's investigation is untouched.
  • No confirmation dialog: undo is the safety, as in herdr's close-tab.

Rigor — operating manual for this sprint

How to execute well. Each value has been adapted to this sprint's specific work.

1 · Intellectual honesty

Calibrate confidence to evidence

"Unbounded depth" is easy to claim and hard to prove. The proof is a test at depth 200, plus a rendered tree panel at depth 40. If virtualisation or indentation degrades somewhere (say depth 60), report the measured limit. Do not write "infinite".

2 · Fairness

Steelman the rejected alternative

Steelman herdr's flat tabs, the operator's current tool. They offer zero learning curve and one strip. The tree wins because a rabbit hole is a tree, and he asked for it explicitly (D6). The cost falls on the shallow Writing user, who sees a tree where a strip would do. The sibling strip is exactly that strip, so a depth-1 tree looks like herdr.

3 · Rigor

Claims are mechanically checkable

Enumerate the number invariants before coding: unique, stable, never reused under a parent, and surviving prune, lift and undo. Encode them two ways. First, enumerate exhaustively every sequence of length ≤ 6 over the four operations (4⁶ + … + 4 = 5,460 sequences). Then run seeded property tests (fast-check, fixed seed, shrinking on failure) over longer sequences with depth up to 200. Record the seed and the sequence-length bound in the test file.

4 · Diligence

Read before writing

Read the investigation tree's recursion (InvestigationSidebar) and parent_investigation_id handling, and reuse its rendering approach. Read windowsStore.ts (8 windows, memory-only) to understand why it is not the tab system: that choice is recorded in the master spec's rejected alternatives. Read components/lemon/LemonToast.tsx: on main its lifetimes are 4000/6000/8000 ms and it has no action or undo slot. Milestone 7 therefore adds an undo action with a 10 000 ms lifetime. Pruning is the most destructive navigation act, so it outlives the longest existing toast (8 s) by the time it takes to read the pruned-subtree summary. Record that reasoning at the constant. If W2's controls wave adds a toast action first, reuse it and cite its branch.

5 · Defensibility

Decisions survive turnover

The tab tree and the research branch are separate records. Put a comment at the top of tabTree.ts saying so, and linking THREAD-CONTRACT §2.2, so nobody later "simplifies" by deleting a branch when a tab is pruned. Point it at the M7 regression test by name ("a pruned research tab's investigation is untouched"). The tab ≠ branch rule must also be in the signed contract before merge.
