Rigor — operating manual for this sprint

How to execute well. Each value has been adapted to this sprint's specific work.

1 · Intellectual honesty

Calibrate confidence to evidence

Lane A is built by Claude sessions, so a fresh Opus session is same lineage. It may produce a first score, but that score is provisional until codex gpt-6-sol (or another non-Claude scorer) confirms it. Milestone 3 means this. The score is judged against ≥ 42/48 with zero vetoes, beside the 24/48 two-veto baseline. Report the provisional and the confirmed score separately.

2 · Fairness

Steelman the rejected alternative

Steelman scoring only the new surfaces, since that is where the work went. It loses: the operator uses the whole app, and the audit's baseline was whole-app, so a partial re-score is not comparable. Name who pays: the whole-app score can stay under 42/48 because of defects lane A did not introduce (for example the MascotStation-obscures-content veto in SCORE-BASELINE-full.md). The separate new-surface sub-score is how that cost is made visible rather than hidden.

3 · Rigor

Claims are mechanically checkable

Six mechanical checks. (a) .lane/RESCORE.md sums all 12 dimensions, and every veto row has an explicit PASS or FAIL with evidence. (b) Each score cites a screenshot from the e2e/mothership-walkthrough.spec.ts run or a story id; a score without an evidence path is capped at 1, per CRAFT-RUBRIC.md:3. (c) The story-coverage test fails when any MS-01..MS-10 component lacks a designed state in light or dark at 1280 or 390. (d) axe reports 0 serious or critical violations on new surfaces. (e) A walkthrough step that could not run is written NOT RUN with its reason, never omitted. (f) The fixture really has 20 workstations, 252 tabs, a 174-tab tree and depth > 12, asserted by the fixture's own test.

4 · Diligence

Read before writing

Before scoring, read /Users/slimydog/.claude/jobs/236c36bd/tmp/design-audit/SCORE-BASELINE-full.md: the dimension table from :38 and the veto table from :56. Re-check each named finding, including Library/index.tsx:566-571 (the invented zero W7 fixed) and MascotStation.tsx:103-104 / zIndex.ts:120 (obscures content). Also read ~/.claude/skills/frontend-craft/references/CRAFT-RUBRIC.md (the evidence rule at :3, the vetoes in §3, and the "Not proved" requirement in §5), plus MS-03's switcher-p95 and MS-04's tree-fps gates, before writing .lane/perf.md.

5 · Defensibility

Decisions survive turnover

.lane/RESCORE.md is the record. Per dimension it holds the baseline and new score, the evidence, and the veto results. It also records the scorer's model and lineage, and ends with a "Not proved" section (CRAFT-RUBRIC §5). RESULT.html cites it, and the next audit starts from it rather than from SCORE-BASELINE-full.md.
