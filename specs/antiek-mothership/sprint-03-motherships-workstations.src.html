Goal

The operator can open several workstations, hop between them and between the three motherships by prefix or chord without losing his place, find anything from one switcher, see live thread state in a sidebar, and read an attention inbox.

Routes become /w/:workstationId/:mothership/…, where mothership is research, writing or reading, and the old routes stay as deep links that resolve into a workstation. Each open workstation keeps, per mothership, its last route and layout. The switcher (⌘K / prefix+g) searches workstations, their open tabs, documents and threads. The sidebar (prefix+b) lists workstations with rolled-up thread state. The inbox (prefix+i) lists what needs you.

Rigor — operating manual for this sprint

How to execute well. Each value has been adapted to this sprint's specific work.

1 · Intellectual honesty

Calibrate confidence to evidence

The server side of workstations and sessions belongs to lane B and may not exist when you finish. Everything you prove against the fake adapter is "contract-shaped, local", not "synced". The handoff must separate the two, and the UI must never say "saved to your account" while only localStorage is in play.

2 · Fairness

Steelman the rejected alternative

Steelman one workstation holding Research and Writing tabs side by side, the option recommended in the interview. It needs fewer concepts and one tab strip. The operator ruled for three motherships (D1/D5), and his standing "net-new, not merged" Write-canvas choice points the same way. Record who pays for the three-shell choice: the person who wants to glance at research while writing. They are served by MS-05's docked reader pane, not by merging the shells.

3 · Rigor

Claims are mechanically checkable

Routing is the dangerous part, and it needs two assertions. (a) Every route in App.tsx (about 60 today) except /login and /_panel/:panelId resolves under /w/:id/:mothership, renders once and keeps its params. (b) Those two resolve outside the tree. Add the switch round trip: after research → writing → research, perMothership.research.route and its layout key equal their values before the switch.

4 · Diligence

Read before writing

Read MyResearch.tsx (grouping, attention, unseen), the #3078 attention registry, the #3088 and #3092 diffs (gh pr diff), workspace/persistence.ts, NavRail, SceneChrome and workflowTaxonomy.ts before writing a line. The sidebar and inbox must extend those, never re-implement them.

5 · Defensibility

Decisions survive turnover

The data-source adapter interface is the seam lane B plugs into. Document it at the top of workstations.ts with the contract section it mirrors (§2.1), so when lane B's registry lands, the swap is one file and nobody redesigns the UI. Contract §2.1 is unsigned as of 2026-09-24. A field rename by lane B voids the one-file-swap claim until both lanes sign, so record the adapter-seam decision in DECISIONS.md as well.
