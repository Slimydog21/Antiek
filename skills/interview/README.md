# skills/interview/

Interview-conducting expertise per subject type. Used by the interview
workflow.

## Examples

- `interview-elderly-family-member-early-career/` — Pacing,
  open-questions, memory-prompting for biography capture.
- `interview-technical-founder-hardware/` — Specification-probing,
  follow-up triggers, falsification hooks.
- `interview-domain-expert-quantum/` — Where to push for precision,
  what counts as a hedge worth surfacing.

These are interview-specific process skills; they live here rather than
under `process/` because they're tightly bound to the interview
acquisition path and the interview workflow.
