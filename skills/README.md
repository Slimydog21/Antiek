# skills/

First-class compounding artifacts. Not an afterthought stored under
Hermes profiles — Skills are the mechanism by which human expertise
and accumulated investigation patterns become persistent capability
rather than ephemeral prompts.

## Three categories

- **`domain/`** — Accumulated facts and frameworks per topic area.
  Quantum, defense, AI infrastructure, semiconductor. Updated via
  Phase 8 after each research cycle. Verified by file-diff against
  snapshots; non-growth fires an alert.
- **`process/`** — How to do specific research workflows. "How to
  evaluate a Series B deep-tech investment." "How to conduct a
  falsification round on a contested thesis." Procedural artifacts
  refined through use. When the orchestrator notices a pattern
  re-derived three times across investigations, it proposes
  codifying it as a process skill. Proposals require human review.
- **`verification/`** — How to verify specific claim classes. "How to
  verify a claim about quantum hardware specifications." "How to
  verify a claim about a sovereign wealth fund's portfolio composition."
  Deterministic-rubric-plus-judged-rubric combinations captured as
  reusable artifacts rather than embedded in role prompts.
- **`interview/`** — Interview-conducting expertise per subject type.
  How to interview a technical founder about a hardware product. How
  to interview an elderly family member about their early career.
  Used by the interview workflow.

## Versioning

Skills carry their own version (e.g., `quantum-knowledge@Q3.7`,
`evaluate-Series-B@P2.1`, `verify-quantum-hardware-claims@V1.4`).
Archived syntheses record the full version triple used at synthesis
time. Backtests against outcome cohorts can correlate skill versions
to outcomes; skill quality becomes measurable, not aspirational.

## Self-improvement (deferred)

The orchestrator can propose new process skills based on observed
re-derivation patterns. Proposals route through `compounding/skill_growth/`
to human review. Fully autonomous skill writing is gated until the
proposals have demonstrated quality.
