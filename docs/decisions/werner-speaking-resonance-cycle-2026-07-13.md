## Decision

Canonical Speak routes use a small brass-and-aurora listening microphone as
their cursor instrument. Werner remains fixed at his station. The instrument is
decorative HTML/CSS and carries no microphone, recording, transcript, consent,
navigation, network, or spend capability.

biography paths receive `speaking-resonance`; Research/Read retain the lens;
Write retains the nib; shared and unknown paths retain ice fishing.
