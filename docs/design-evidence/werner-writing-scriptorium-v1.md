## Intent

Explore a calm alpine scriptorium in which Werner remains seated at his fixed
home while a small brass fountain-pen nib becomes the operator's writing
instrument. A fine aurora filament relates station and pointer without making
Werner chase the cursor. Blank cream paper, brass tools, books, snow, and blue
mountains establish a tactile editorial atmosphere rather than a SaaS panel.

## Authority boundary

This PNG is presentation and design evidence only. It does not prove product
behavior, editable text, citations, provenance, accessibility, route semantics,
cursor tracking, responsive layout, or saved writing. Its blank paper must not
be treated as a document surface and its pixels must never replace the working
HTML editor.

The implemented product translation is HTML-native:

- `WritingNibCursor.tsx` implements the pointer instrument.
- `writing-nib.css` implements its tokenized brass-and-aurora grammar.
- `WritingNibCursor.stories.tsx` implements the observable scriptorium using
  real, selectable HTML.
- `activities/selection.ts` assigns the metaphor only to taxonomy Write routes.

## Prompt intent summary

Preserve Werner's exact friendly hand-drawn identity. Place him at a warm but
restrained alpine writing desk, connect the station to one small brass nib with
a thin teal filament, keep papers blank, and use Antiek's ice, ink, sun, aurora,
and brass palette. Avoid readable text, fake product data, dashboards,
photorealism, 3D gloss, cyberpunk neon, logos, watermarks, and extra penguins.
