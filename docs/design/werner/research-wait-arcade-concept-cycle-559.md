monitor. Preserve canonical Werner. Keep live research slips, costs, states, steering, and

## Accepted design decisions

- The brass trace rail is semantic: it reports the number of researches still running.
- The drawer is subordinate to the monitor and never replaces research cards.
- The lemon diamond is the single concentrated accent; surrounding chrome stays glacial.
- The canvas is contained inside a specimen tray and collapses to one column at narrow widths.

## Rejected image decisions

- The generated bitmap is not used as a layout, background, hotspot map, or runtime mascot.
- The large visual density is reduced in product code so the existing authoritative monitor
