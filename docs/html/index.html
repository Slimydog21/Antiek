Antiek — Exa & Browserbase integration and sprint analyses

This is the operator-facing landing page for the Antiek × Exa × Browserbase integration spec (2026-05-21) and the comprehensive analysis of Sprints 17 through 22+ that the spec's wedge placement depends on.

The spec defends a single architectural decision: Exa and Browserbase are different layers. Exa is the discovery layer (query → URLs); Browserbase is an ingestion-layer escalation fallback (when httpx can't reach the content). They share no adapter, no config namespace, no environment variable prefix. Both flow through the existing substrate-write seam at acquisition/urls/adapter.ingest_url(…).

The sprint pages translate the wedge placement in §16 of the spec into the broader sprint context — what each sprint ships in total, why the sequencing is binding, and what's deliberately deferred to a later sprint.

Reading order

For an operator approaching this material fresh:

  1. Read the spec, §3 (the single architectural decision) first — the wedges below it only make sense once that split is internalized.
  2. Then §5 (verdict matrix) for the at-a-glance.
  3. Then Sprint 18 — the legal gate is the precondition the spec hangs on.
  4. Then Sprint 19 — where the two INTEGRATE NOW wedges actually land.
  5. Then Sprint 20 onward for the PHASE 2 / DEFER trajectory.

For an implementing agent picking up Wedge 1 or Wedge 2: read the spec end-to-end, then Sprint 18 and Sprint 19 for the surrounding substrate context. The wedge work cannot land without the legal gate from Sprint 18 in production.
