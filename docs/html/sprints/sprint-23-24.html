1. Binding ads gate #

Phase 4 ads gate

Sprints 23-24 ship only after: Sprint 22 multi-user is stable and hardened against graph contamination; at least one creator and one publisher are accruing under the attribution pipeline with no rev-share routed yet; and the anti-gaming layer passes documented external adversarial review. Real money raises the cost of every attribution or fraud error.

2. Progressive phases #

2.1 Vertical ad slot mechanic

Ads appear as page-border banners, not inline with synthesis prose. Topic classification chooses sector, sub-sector, and audience-intent dimensions. No programmatic bidding ships here; inventory is manually curated and lead-gen oriented.

Exit: ads render on real public-graph pages without breaking the notebook aesthetic; voice-style score on ad-bearing pages stays within 5% of non-ad pages.

2.2 Attribution to rev-share payout pipeline

Ad revenue routes through the attribution system. The platform takes 30%; the IP-holder share is 70%; creator and publisher paths respect opt-in and payout eligibility. Stripe Connect extends from billing into rev-share payouts with credit-only versus payout-eligible account state.

Exit: a first creator receives a non-trivial monthly payout through Stripe Connect and the attribution trail is auditable end to end.

2.3 Anti-gaming layer

Click-fraud, view-fraud, and attribution-gaming detectors catch bot traffic, session bursts, deep attribution chains, single-consumer dominance, and creator-cluster collusion before money routes. External red-team review is binding.

Exit: simulated fraud attempts are caught before revenue routes and the external red-team report is filed.

2.4 Minimum payout threshold, KYC, and tax reporting

The payout threshold is $10. Below-threshold balances roll over. KYC and 1099 reporting are required for creators above threshold. Stripe Connect onboarding remains the KYC rail; substrate tax-reporting support aggregates payout totals for operator/accountant review.

Exit: a threshold-crossing creator completes KYC and receives payout; below-threshold rollover behaves correctly on a synthetic 13-month test.

2.5 Lead-gen advertiser onboarding

The first advertiser wave is manual and operator-driven. Vertical SaaS, consulting firms, and recruiting platforms are targeted by topic. The operator-only advertiser console handles inventory, targeting rules, creative upload, and campaign reporting.

Exit: at least three vertical advertisers are paying for ad slots and can see campaign performance through operator-mediated reports.

2.6 Red-team report and voice/style audit

Two pre-launch reviews are required: external anti-gaming review across botnet view inflation, attribution graph injection, and multi-account self-attribution; and an internal voice/style audit comparing ad-bearing pages against baseline.

Exit: both reports pass and legal counsel signs off on the KYC/1099 posture.

5. Acceptance & exit criteria #

  • At least one creator receives a real payout above the threshold through Stripe.
  • At least three advertisers are paying, with monthly run-rate above $5K.
  • A sampled audit of 20 payouts shows no operator-reversed attributions.
  • External red-team report catches the required attack classes pre-payout.
  • Voice/style audit shows less than 5% regression versus non-ad baseline.
  • Legal counsel has reviewed the KYC and 1099 posture; no above-threshold payout happens without KYC.
