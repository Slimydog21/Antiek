1. Sprint thesis #

Sprint 17 is structurally unusual: instead of one big engineering push, it distributes across six independent workstreams whose failure modes do not cross. The interview-voice mainline is engineering-heavy; the dispatch tier measurement is a one-line config change with a two-week observation window; the four integration-spec items are each ~half a day. A blocker on any one of them does not block the others.

Why this shape: Sprint 16 ratified the Hermes verdict at commit cd602c9; with the bridge stable, the operator has appetite for a wider-but-shallower sprint that closes cheap unlocks across the integration spec backlog (PostHog Wedge 1a/1b, autoresearch INTEGRATE NOW, Prime F+D debt) while a substantial new product surface (interview voice) builds in parallel. The risk pattern: the integration items take less time than projected, freeing capacity for the Brainstorming Workstation scaffold (the operator-preferred product surface per master-spec §4.5).

2. Mainline — interview voice mode (Loop 4) #

Reference: master-product-spec.md §11.5 and §11.6. The substantive engineering build of the sprint.

2.1 What it is

WebRTC capture + TTS dispatch tier + streaming Whisper transcription, wired into a new orchestration loop — Loop 4 (Interview) — parallel to Loop 1 (research), Loop 2 (wrestling), and Loop 3 (RL training, deferred). The interview surface is the operator's preferred surface for the autobiography product validation: a real-time conversation with the AI interviewer that produces a transcript flowing through acquisition/voice/ into the note-taking pipeline.

2.2 What this is NOT (Sprint 17)

  • Synchronous voice (OpenAI Realtime API). Deferred to Sprint 23+ per master-spec §15.3. The realistic round-trip latency for the async pattern is 3–5s, validated as workable for the biography use case.
  • Voice over the dispatch tier. TTS becomes a dispatch tier in its own right with its own configuration; this is the right architectural move but introduces a new tier that the cost-tracking and weekly-report code must learn about.
  • Voice-driven actions. Saying "open Claude's pricing page" doesn't navigate the UI. That is closer to Wedge 4 of the PostHog spec (ubiquitous AI assistant) and is Sprint 19–20 work.

2.3 Acceptance criteria

Operator can send an interview link to themselves, record a 5-minute conversation in voice mode, receive a transcript that flows through acquisition/voice/ into the note-taking pipeline, and see insights + open questions surface in the workstation.

3. Substrate — dispatch tier-differentiation measurement gate #

Reference: master-product-spec.md §14.4. Substrate-wide measurement, two-week observation window opening Sprint 17 and closing Sprint 20.

3.1 The question being measured

The Hermes-primary posture (Grok 4.3 verify-tier; DeepSeek V4 pro/flash) is correct for most tiers but under measurement for synthesis. Synthesis is the operator-facing artifact; the voice/style discipline at §5 is synthesizer-level. Grok 4.3 has not been benchmarked on long-form English research writing the way Opus 4.7 has, and the master spec's discipline is to measure before deciding.

3.2 Protocol (verbatim from master §14.4)

  1. Pin synthesizer tier to Opus 4.7 via OpenRouter primary, Hermes fallback. Edit at substrate/dispatch/config.yaml.
  2. Run 2 weeks of normal investigation traffic.
  3. Measure verifier pass rates per provider on synthesis outputs.
  4. Verdict at Sprint 20:
    • Within 5pp of Opus → flip back to Hermes primary on cost grounds.
    • Larger gap → keep Opus on synthesis as primary; the cost savings on synthesis are illusory because they convert to additional verifier and re-synthesizer dispatch.

3.3 Why this matters to the Exa / Browserbase spec

The dispatch tier verdict at Sprint 20 informs whether the verifier tier — the consumer of Wedge 3's exa_lookup_claim tool — is Grok 4.3 (Hermes) or shifted. The Wedge 3 calibration eval set (§15.3 of the spec) is verifier-specific; a mid-sprint tier change would invalidate the calibration. Sprint 17 starts the measurement so Sprint 20 can land it cleanly.

4. program.md per role (autoresearch INTEGRATE NOW) #

Half a day. Reference: integration_autoresearch.md — the one INTEGRATE NOW item.

4.1 What it is

Each of the 12 roles at roles/<role>/ gets a program.md file written in plain markdown. The format follows the autoresearch pattern (Karpathy 2026-03): lightweight, operator-editable instructions naming what the role does, what good output looks like, what to avoid, and what hypotheses to try when iterating.

4.2 Why this lands first in the sprint

It is the mutation target for the prompt autoresearch loop (autoresearch Wedge 1, Sprint 19+ parallel side-track). Without program.md per role, the autoresearch runner has no surface to write the hypothesis against. Half-day cost, zero production risk, unlocks the entire Wedge-1 chain.

4.3 Roles shipped this sprint (12 total)

decomposer, evidence_retriever, parameter_extractor, connector, synthesizer, challenger, grounder, note_taker, user_agent, creative_writer, interviewer, voice_note_followup.

4.4 Acceptance criteria

  • 12 program.md files committed.
  • The synthesizer's program.md explicitly codifies the voice/style discipline from master §5.1–§5.5.
  • Codegen and tests still passing.

6. Lemon UI evaluation spike (PostHog Wedge 1b) #

Half a day on a side branch. Reference: integration_posthog.md §5.1b. The decision is the deliverable, not the adoption.

6.1 What it is

npm i @posthog/lemon-ui in a branch; replace one component (ChatInput is lowest-risk) and measure against four decision criteria:

  • Bundle size delta < 80 KB gzipped.
  • TypeScript strict compile clean.
  • Tailwind interop verified.
  • Operator's visual-fit eye test passes (researcher's-notebook aesthetic preserved per master §5.5).
  • At least 60% migration coverage projection across the existing component surface.

6.2 The two defensible outcomes

VerdictWhat happens
ADOPT (all four gates passed)Merge the migration to main; plan Sprint 18 work for the remaining components.
KEEP CUSTOM (any gate failed)Document the failing gate(s); spike branch is deleted; Storybook stays as the design-system source of truth without Lemon UI underneath.

Either verdict is defensible. The default assumption is KEEP CUSTOM — Lemon UI is built for PostHog's SaaS-dashboard aesthetic and the default expectation is that it will not pass the eye test. The spike's job is to verify that assumption empirically rather than assert it.

9. Order of operations #

Operator-recommended sequence (each item's failure modes are independent; sequencing is by leverage + risk):

  1. program.md per role — half-day, no production impact, unlocks Sprint 19 autoresearch Wedge 1. Lowest risk, highest unlock value.
  2. Storybook scaffold — half-day, no production impact, unlocks Sprint 18 notebook surface work. Pure-upside hygiene.
  3. Lemon UI evaluation spike — half-day on a branch, no merge required to mainline. Decision-making work.
  4. Dispatch tier-differentiation measurement begins — one config edit; 2-week measurement window starts. Substrate change, low risk, gates Sprint 20 verdict.
  5. Interview voice mode — mainline sprint work, the substantive build.
  6. Brainstorming Workstation scaffold — operator-preferred direction; may slip to Sprint 18 if voice-mode work consumes more capacity than projected.

Items 1–4 are designed to land in the first 2 days. Item 5 takes the remainder of the sprint. Item 6 lands in the last 1–2 days OR slips to Sprint 18.
