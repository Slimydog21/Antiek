1. Strategic objective #

Sprint 21 is split into two independent workstreams. The first is the Synquery expert-network integration that slipped from Sprint 18 because publisher onboarding and expert sourcing have unrelated workflows and failure modes. Synquery activates only after creation-surface PMF signal that operators want to commission expert calls. The second is Phase 8 enforcing, conditional on Sprint 20's Wedge 1 ratification verdict.

2. Progressive phases #

2.1 Creation-surface PMF gate check

The operator decides whether the creation surface has produced enough demand-shape for expert calls. Inputs include distinct deliverables produced, surviving open questions that require interviews, and willingness to pay for expert calls.

Exit: gate verdict is on paper. Pass proceeds to Synquery activation; fail reschedules Synquery.

2.2 Synquery API client

Per §11.6, the substrate identifies questions where expert interviews are more appropriate than autonomous research. The operator clicks find-an-expert, Synquery returns matching experts and booking flow, the interview happens through Synquery, and the transcript returns to Antiek as a new ingested tier-2 source.

Exit: operator can find and book an expert through Antiek; transcript ingests into the substrate as a tier-2 source.

2.3 Phase 8 enforcing mode

Per integration_autoresearch.md Wedge 2, Phase 8's skill-patch accept/reject gate flips from shadow to enforcing. Minimum cohort size stays at 50. Failed patches are rejected at the patch-application step and their rejection reasons are recorded as typed events.

Exit: at least one real skill patch has been rejected by the enforcing gate and the operator agrees the rejection was correct.

2.4 Ubiquitous AI across all surfaces

The Sprint 19 AI sidecar broadens across research workstation, document wrestle, notebook, brainstorming workstation, creation studio, and interview surfaces. The same context-aware capabilities and UI-action undo affordance flow through the event log.

Exit: the AI sidecar is present and functional on every operator surface.

2.5 Synquery cost discipline

Expert calls are expensive. The intended cap model defaults to a per-deliverable Synquery spend cap of $1,000, operator-overridable and hard-capped at $5,000. Cost events must flow through the same cost discipline that protects the rest of the substrate.

Exit: a Synquery booking is enforced against the cap and an over-cap attempt is blocked at the API surface.
