1. Strategic objective #

Sprint 20 resolves three binary outcomes:

  1. Visible-artifact growth motion opens. The operator publishes outputs from Antiek under any byline or account. Peers ask what tool produced them. Individual-to-individual organic discovery either starts or does not.
  2. Dispatch tier-measurement verdict per §14.4. Two weeks of synthesizer-on-Opus traffic versus Hermes baseline decides whether to flip synthesis back to Hermes-primary on cost grounds or keep Opus-primary on quality grounds.
  3. Autoresearch Wedge 1 ratification verdict per §15.6. The operator reads Sprint 19's local prompt mutation report and decides whether the mutations clear the Lutke-gap test.

2. Progressive phases #

2.1 Operator publishes Antiek-produced outputs into the world

Per §9.0.1 / §13.4, the operator uses Antiek and publishes a substantive output under their personal account, Sanabil byline, or another chosen byline. The output must surface the voice/style discipline at a quality that distinguishes it from generic LLM output.

Exit: at least one substantive piece has been published outside Antiek.

2.2 Trajectory replay viewer — PostHog Wedge 5

A timeline scrubber and event-at-time renderer for re-reading investigations, watch-for-later trajectories, and operator-graded outcomes. This deliberately does not vendor rrweb: Antiek records typed events, not DOM mutations. The notebook remains the render target.

Exit: a past investigation can be scrubbed through its lifecycle and render meaningfully different intermediate states.

2.3 Dispatch tier-differentiation verdict

The end-of-window report compares verifier pass rates and cost-per-acceptable-synthesis per provider. The operator signs off on Hermes-back-to-primary or Opus-stays-primary. The verdict reshapes substrate/dispatch/config.yaml for the rest of the roadmap.

Exit: dispatch posture is decided and committed for the synthesizer tier.

2.4 Autoresearch Wedge 1 ratify-or-reject

The operator reads the Sprint 19 prompt-autoresearch mutation report and decides whether the proposed synthesizer prompts are distinguishably better than the existing prompts. Ratification unlocks Wedges 2–4; rejection drops them and keeps Phase 8 on its current unconditional patching path.

Exit: the verdict is on paper, signed by the operator, and reflected in the Sprint 21 plan.

2.5 Phase 8 gate shadow-mode

If Wedge 1 ratifies, compounding/skill_growth/gate.py runs the Phase 8 accept/reject gate in shadow mode: it computes and logs the decision but does not block patches. Minimum cohort size is 50; shadow notes carry the would-be decision for calibration.

Exit: shadow-mode decisions are calibrated against operator judgment for at least 30 days of Phase 8 patches.
