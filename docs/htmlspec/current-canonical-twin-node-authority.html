Cycle 116. Make a historically retained Cycle 115 insight or question reusable only while its exact owner review, canonical twin, evidence, rights, and takedown authority remain current.

Invariant

Contract

  • Input is an exact coordinator-held graph connection, promotion ledger, twin ledger, owner ID, and candidate ID.
