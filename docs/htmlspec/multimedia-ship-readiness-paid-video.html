MSR-04: Compound paid registered-video authority

Parent program

Spec lineage

Decision

A current registered paid video passes the cost gate only with one compound authority containing the independently signed MSR-01 direct snapshot and MSR-02 production-byte projection at exactly one cutoff. The two totals retain separate labels and are never summed or substituted.

Global invariants

  1. Paid conflict is terminal and never reaches local fallback.
  2. Registered paid video cannot omit either child. Projection-only remains blocked until signed direct-scope absence exists.
  3. Child cutoffs, owner digest, asset, and revision are identical. The projection receipt digest equals the current production link.
  4. Direct and production totals may overlap or differ. No combined total is created.
  5. Production projection, direct snapshot, provider execution, narration, and local-zero signing domains remain independent.
  6. Video/audio registration clears prior hardening. Final persistence reopens current revision and link under the asset-store lock.
  7. The route accepts no body and callers choose no authority, run, receipt, cutoff, key, or total.
  8. All evidence work is read-only until the existing report save.

Refusals

Missing runtime, child authority, direct scope, production link, receipt equality, or paid-audio closure is unavailable. MAC, identity, cutoff, key-domain, cardinality, stale-state, or persisted-link contradiction is conflict. Neither becomes local zero.

Out of scope

No paid-audio byte closure, direct-scope absence authority, mixed allocation, refund accounting, learned routing, rights automation, provider call, production, or publication.

Harness hint

Land 04A before 04B/04C; integrate 04B and 04C; then use 04D to refute downgrade, stale-state, accounting, and compatibility claims.
