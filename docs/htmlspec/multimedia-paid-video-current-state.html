MSR-04C: Persist only current production authority

Parent MSR-04

Spec lineage

    Registration invalidation

    First attachment of either video or audio production link clears any prior hardening report in the same exclusive-lock update. Exact attachment replay remains idempotent and does not erase a report created after registration.

    Final compare-and-set

    run_hardening reopens the current owner-bound record under its existing exclusive lock. For a compound paid-video bundle it requires the same revision, a current video production link, exact projection receipt digest equality, no audio link, and valid child evidence under distinct keys before saving. A link or revision change is conflict and writes nothing.

    Read model

    Summary hardening status derives from the current record only. Legacy reports still parse. Registration and steering cannot expose a stale report as current.

    Tests

    • Pre-registration report is cleared by first video and audio attachment.
    • Exact attachment replay preserves a post-registration report.
    • Changed link/revision during evidence construction prevents persistence.
    • Foreign owner, wrong receipt, paid bundle on audio, local evidence mixed with paid, and projection-only fail closed.
    • Failed attempts leave the account JSON byte-identical.
