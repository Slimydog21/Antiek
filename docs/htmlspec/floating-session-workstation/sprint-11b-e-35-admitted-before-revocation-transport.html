Linearizable admitted-before-revocation transport

Make the quarantined unified checkpoint prove a paid request was admitted before revocation, then claim at most one test-only transport attempt with honest ambiguity accounting.

Parent context and evidence

Cycle 34 placed capability/revocation, source provenance, owner operation, consent, queue lease, budget, logical effect, paid admission, hold, attempt, and append-only transition schemas in one SQLite WAL root. Its certified 34E boundary deliberately kept marker commit, pinned epoch-one open, forward recovery, runtime writers, admission, and transition closed. Goal and success criteria

  • All mutable eligibility writers and admission serialize through the exact unified store using BEGIN IMMEDIATE; no lock federation, ATTACH, saga, mirror, or reread is called atomic.
  • The admission COMMIT is the admitted-before-revocation linearization point. Authority drift committed first rejects; drift committed later preserves the admitted history.
  • Cancellation-before-first-attempt and attempt claim are a second exact serialized race. A cancellation winner yields zero calls; a claim winner persists before adapter entry.
  • No DB/file/process lock crosses the spy call. Every post-claim ambiguity becomes absorbing paid_unknown; no automatic redispatch.
  • The named xfail becomes an ordinary deterministic two-order test only after all state, crash, leakage, import, and accounting gates pass.

Authority, time, outcomes, and accounting

Honest permit authority

QuarantinedTestTransportPermitV1 is not a public DTO: it is an intentionally authority-conferring, narrowly scoped synthetic test capability. It confers exactly one call through the reviewed tests/support acceptor and no production/provider/checkpoint/sink/transition authority. Only claim COMMIT plus exact reread may mint it; no DTO, serialized/copy/subclass/fork/reopened-store/reconstructed value substitutes.

Trusted time and bounded claim entitlement

Public callers do not choose transition timestamps. Admission, cancellation, claim, and outcome all read an injected-at-construction, contract-bound trusted clock; fixture clocks are authorized and strictly nondecreasing. Capability expiry, request horizon, and bounded consent validity each freeze their own inclusive/exclusive predicate. Lease is always half-open: claim requires trusted_now < lease_exclusive_until_ms. Admission persists every raw bound and the derived last-valid instant inside the store-encrypted canonical UnifiedPaidAdmissionCandidateV2 ciphertext, whose digest and AEAD AAD bind the admission row. This preserves the frozen 18-table SQL: claim authenticates/decrypts that exact row, recomputes every predicate with a fresh reading, and rejects rollback/backdating, empty bounds, DB edits, and ciphertext substitution.

Attempt-bound receipt and permit crash rule

The store creates the permit through a closed constructor and registers its object identity in a per-store/per-boot one-shot registry protected by a 256-bit random memory-only HMAC key generated at store construction. Atomic consume CASes issued → consumed_pre_entry and yields a non-transferable entry token; the acceptor CASes consumed_pre_entry → adapter_entered immediately before invoking the spy. Only consumed_pre_entry can mint a MAC-verified terminal no-network receipt. After entry/return, the store-owned permit method mints QuarantinedTestTransportOutcomeReceiptV1 with HMAC-SHA-256 over domain antiek.midnight-oil.private-paid-test-transport-outcome-receipt.v1\0 and store/boot/permit/admission/effect/attempt/ordinal, request/idempotency blind IDs, entry sequence, disposition, response digest/length, charge evidence, and occurrence time. Verification requires the exact live store/boot registry entry, constant-time MAC verification, expected state, and one-shot unconsumed → consumed CAS. Constructors, key, and registry mutation are inaccessible to callers. Crash before entry without a MACed no-network receipt, or any crash after entry, becomes unknown; there is no permit reconstruction or redispatch.

Returned-byte continuation

A returned receipt commits encrypted response bytes before acknowledging the acceptor. Add open_quarantined_returned_response(..., exact owner/effect/attempt/state-version authority) → QuarantinedReturnedResponseHandleV1: a process-bound one-shot decrypting handle for the checker, never a DTO or transport permit. Crash/reopen can reissue it only after exact owner authentication and durable returned evidence; consumption advances no state, and settlement CAS prevents duplicate accounting.
