Owner-authorized Substack excerpts

Give a confirmed collective exactly one bounded, owner-selected Substack excerpt for private model context without fetching a page or feed, forwarding credentials, relabeling paid text as open content, or laundering it into a public deliverable.

spec readymilestone 1 implementedproduction remains disabled4 milestones remain

Parent context and terminal decision

FSW-SPR-11 now ships a signed fixed-egress arXiv abstract connector. It deliberately rejects every Substack reference in substrate/midnight_oil/publication_sources.py. Existing Substack RSS ingest and interactive hydrate are different products: they accept generic feed/post clients, may hold full bodies, use heuristic paywall detection, and do not prove exact collective, excerpt, provider-use, expiry, or revocation authority.

Decision: V1 performs zero Substack network egress. The browser/operator supplies only a selected bounded excerpt and an attestation about the representation and range. The server creates two immutable owner-private artifacts: a revocable use authorization and a content-addressed excerpt receipt. Midnight Oil reads only the exact receipt bytes.
Non-negotiable: A reviewed URL proves source identity only. A subscription proves access only. Neither proves permission to send a full paid post to a model. V1 never stores a Substack cookie, password, bearer/session header, page HTML, full paid body, or hidden remainder.

Authority flow

1 · Exact ref
Confirmed collective pins ref_id, exact *.substack.com/p/<slug>, and preview hash.
→
2 · Use grant
Owner attests lawful access and requests private processing under closed constraints; a separate provider capability must prove route compatibility.
→
3 · Excerpt receipt
Only bounded UTF-8 text, representation evidence, range, and hashes enter owner-private storage.
→
4 · Reviewed preview
Collective preview changes and shows bytes, expiry, private-output consequence, and receipt hash.
→
5 · Paid dispatch
Worker rereads the exact receipt and checks owner, revision, expiry, and revocation immediately before every model call.

Five-values operating manual

1 · Intellectual honesty

Owner attestation is evidence of the owner's decision, not publisher permission or proof of the unseen full representation. UI and receipts say exactly that. Paid text is never called T1, open, redistributable, or full-text.

2 · Fairness

The owner gets private research utility from a small selection. The publisher does not absorb page scraping, credential forwarding, paywall bypass, public rehosting, model-training export, or silent full-body transmission.

3 · Rigor

Test every identity, byte, range, hash, expiry, revocation, crash, replay, substitution, and output-taint boundary. Provider-call count is zero for every failed prerequisite and exactly authorized for a valid run.

4 · Diligence

Reuse owner stores, confirmed collective revisions, consent CAS, durable source barrier, and personal-reading gates. Do not reuse hydrate exception swallowing, RSS redirect/fetch behavior, or generic URL plumbing.

5 · Defensibility

An auditor can reconstruct who authorized what excerpt, for which immutable collective, under what private-use policy, for how long, whether it was revoked, what exact bytes reached a provider, and why no public consumer received them.

Rejected alternatives

  • Remove the current Substack guard and reuse RSS/hydrate: rejected; those lanes fetch generic/full material, follow redirects, use heuristic truncation, and carry no spend authority.
  • Owner checkbox plus URL: rejected; identity and access do not prove bounded content or provider use.
  • Fetch full page then truncate: rejected; the system already acquired unauthorized excess bytes and may have bypassed a paywall.
  • Byte cap without representation/range attestation: rejected; a short complete post can fit under the cap. V1 additionally rejects an attested whole-representation range, while honestly acknowledging that a dishonest attestation cannot be detected without source bytes.
  • DOM character offsets: rejected; mutable DOM/extraction makes them non-reproducible. V1 records owner-attested UTF-8 byte offsets as unverified evidence, not publisher proof.
  • Call paid text T1: rejected; T1 is open/redistributable arXiv evidence. Substack V1 is owner-private and non-attributable.
  • Replay cached excerpt after revocation: rejected; content-use authority differs from connector egress authority and must remain live for each provider call.
  • Put URL/ref/range hashes in general audit: rejected; predictable identifiers reveal reading interests. Use per-install keyed correlation or isolated short-retention security audit only.
