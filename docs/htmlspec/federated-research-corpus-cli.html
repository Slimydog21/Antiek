One offline research corpus over durable scholarly sources

The operator explicitly mounts one or more reviewed S2, OpenAlex, and CORE authorities. Search queries every mount and returns collision-safe mount:opaque-id identifiers. Fetch delegates to the exact source and preserves its provenance and rights.

Ranking

Provider-local scores are not assumed comparable. Results use deterministic reciprocal-rank fusion with k=60, then source-qualified ID for ties. A failing source aborts rather than silently shrinking the evidence universe.

Runtime contract

  • --mount KIND=PATH is explicit and repeatable; kinds are unique and limited to s2, openalex, and core.
  • Every authority must already exist and pass its own schema, digest, integrity, path, and record validation.
  • search is bounded to 1–300 results. fetch requires a qualified identifier and preserves the underlying document provenance.
  • No command performs a provider call, sync, graph write, event, or app mutation.
