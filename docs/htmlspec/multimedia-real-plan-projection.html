Projection

A pure defensive adapter validates suggestions, omissions, chapter identities and durations, script identity/order, scene-to-chapter references, unsourced-line ledger, and citation chunk/document identities. It derives chapter transcript only from matching persisted script lines, visual status from persisted scenes and citations, and source cards only from persisted citations.

Honest states

Before persistence, an offline example may remain but is explicitly labeled and cannot be approved. Once an asset exists, malformed or incomplete plan data produces a visible non-approvable error; fixture suggestions, chapters, transcript, sources, omissions, claims, and costs never fill a missing server field.

Revision behavior

Create, reopen, and steer replace the entire projected plan. Active chapter and source selection survive only when their identities exist in the current revision; otherwise selection moves to the first persisted identity or clears.

Acceptance

  • A server plan with unrelated content renders only that content.
  • Suggestions, omissions, storyboard, transcript, unsourced reasons, and source cards are server-derived.
  • Malformed arrays, duplicate IDs, missing scripts, detached scenes, invalid citations, and conflicting unsourced ledgers fail visibly.
  • Offline fixture is labeled example-only and approval remains disabled.
  • No provider/model call, backend planner change, publication, merge, or deploy.
