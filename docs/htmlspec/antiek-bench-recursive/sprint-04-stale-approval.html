SPR-04 — Stale-safe proposal approval

← master

Make the operator gate compare-and-promote: recursive learning must never roll evaluation truth back over a newer approved suite.

Contract

  1. Persist a deterministic digest over proposal identity and complete suite content.
  2. Approve only when the proposal is proposed and its base equals the active suite.
  3. Mark a base mismatch stale; leave registry and suite map unchanged.
  4. Approved replay is idempotent and never re-promotes after another suite becomes active.
  5. Rejected and stale proposals are terminal; tampered payloads fail before registry mutation.
  6. Persist approving intent before promotion; replay reconciles a completed registry mutation after a final store-write failure.
  7. Legacy proposals without an immutable digest require explicit migration and cannot be mistaken for current tampering.

Acceptance

  • Two proposals from the same base: approving one makes the other stale.
  • Replaying the first approval after a later promotion cannot roll back the active suite.
  • Changed base, proposed version, item, prompt, keywords, or digest cannot promote.
  • Settings JSON/HTML reports stale/tampered outcomes honestly with promoted=false.

Non-goals

No automatic merge of competing proposals, no silent rebasing, and no routing authority.
