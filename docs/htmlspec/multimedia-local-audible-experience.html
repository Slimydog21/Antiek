Make movement-friendly audible learning a first-class local product

Executable child spec of the multimedia master and cheapest-local workstation. Antiek already has a rigorous AudibleRunManifest for signposts, remember beats, recaps, grounded learned claims, chapter windows, and follow-up prompts. This sprint replaces its fake/generic synthesis path with trusted local chapter WAV authority and gives audio-only assets their own durable receipt, verified byte-range playback, exact-revision registration, and workstation controls.

Product outcome

A user can create a 15–45 minute audio experience for the gym, run, commute, or focused listening. The plan preserves sourced factual claims, inserts concise navigation and retention beats, synthesizes locally at exactly $0, exposes chapter seeking and source-linked learned claims, and supports text/voice steering through normal revision creation. It never creates a dummy video or routes audio through an educational-video receipt.

Authority chain

  1. Require an authenticated current ready cheapest audio_experience revision with fully grounded factual narration.
  2. Transform the canonical plan through prepare_audible_run_plan; bind the transformed plan digest, marker identities, learned claims, and original source authority.
  3. Derive one canonical PreparedChapterTTSRequest per transformed spoken chapter and execute/reopen through LocalTTSAdapter.
  4. Bridge exact verified chapter WAVs into produce_narration_track; bind measured chapter and total durations.
  5. Compile and seal the existing AudibleRunManifest from the transformed plan and the verified chapter timing. No fake bytes, provider calls, or estimated duration enter production.
  6. Issue a new MAC-protected AudibleExperienceReceipt binding owner, asset, revision, local narration artifact, audible-run artifact, transcript/marker/learned-claim authority, zero cost, and private publication paths.
  7. Open through a dedicated verified-audio runtime, serve authenticated WAV byte ranges and safe chapter/marker/learned-claim metadata, then attach an idempotent audio production link to only the still-current revision.

Durability

Use a separate MAC-protected phase ledger: preparing, narration-producing, narration-succeeded, receipting, succeeded, registered. External local synthesis and FFmpeg concatenation use explicit unknown-outcome recovery; receipt issuance and registration converge only on exact authorities. Generic retry cannot reinterpret an ambiguous phase. Steering invalidates current registration for the new revision while historical receipts remain reopenable.

Receipt and playback

The audio receipt is not a relaxed video receipt. It has no visual packet, dimensions, captions stream, or MP4. Verified metadata includes receipt/audio digests, byte count, measured duration, ordered chapter windows, retention markers, learned claims, and source counts. HTTP responses never contain local paths, keys, signatures, raw graph snapshots, or internal digests beyond existing playback validators. Byte reads use the same private descriptor snapshot discipline as verified video playback.

Workstation

  • For cheapest audio assets, show Local audible experience · $0 instead of the documentary panel.
  • Preflight local speech, ffmpeg/ffprobe, private storage, receipt authority, and playback.
  • Prepare and recover commands carry only expected revision and opaque prepared-set identity.
  • Show chapter duration, narration readiness, source count, remember/recap readiness, and learned-claim count. Do not expose authority IDs or digests.
  • The primary command is Produce audible experience · $0; unknown outcomes replace it with Recover audible experience.
  • Registered output opens the existing audio player with chapter seeking. A learned-claims drawer offers source review and follow-up research prompts suitable after the run.

Non-goals

No podcast dialogue generation, music, sound effects, paid TTS, video placeholder, Krea, automatic web research, automatic steering merge, or voice-input implementation in this sprint. The API and UI reserve steering seams but reuse the existing revision system.

Acceptance

  • A real macOS fixture creates multiple local chapter WAVs, one canonical PCM narration track, one audible-run artifact, one audio receipt, and authenticated byte-range playback with exactly zero spend and zero network/provider calls.
  • Signpost/remember/recap/learned-claim timing and source authority exactly match measured chapter audio; tamper and unsourced content fail closed.
  • Exact replay invokes no second synthesis or concatenation. Injected crashes at every external boundary require and pass explicit recovery without duplicate work.
  • Foreign, stale, unready, video, non-cheapest, changed-plan, malformed marker, DB/MAC/file/path/mode/digest/executable, and conflicting registration cases fail.
  • Frontend tests cover audio-only capability, progress, recovery replacement, chapter seeking metadata, learned claims, stale responses, and corrected no-paid-fallback copy.
  • Broad backend/frontend suites, typecheck/build, real audio probe, HardenX, independent review, and desktop/mobile browser QA complete the slice.
