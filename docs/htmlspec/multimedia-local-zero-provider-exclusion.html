MSR-03C: Paid Provider Row Exclusion

Parent MSR-03

Spec lineage

Leaf sprint under MSR-03.

    Contract

    A local-zero claim is eligible only when one read transaction finds no provider execution row in its explicit scope. Any row blocks zero regardless of status, settlement, or charge because mixed paid/local allocation is not implemented.

    Scope

    • Video scope is the exact parent revision plus every deterministic narration child revision derived from the verified receipt's ordered chapter sources.
    • Audio scope is the exact parent revision. No provider child-revision convention exists for AudibleRun in v1; the evidence names this limitation.
    • Queries bind exact asset_id and revision IDs. Other owners, assets, historical parent revisions, and unrelated children are not absorbed.
    • The builder receives revision scope from reopened local authority, never from request JSON.

    Database semantics

    • Missing database or missing multimedia_provider_executions table is evidence_unavailable, not zero.
    • One or more rows is evidence_conflict. Do not decode, settle, reconcile, delete, or reinterpret the rows.
    • Record sorted excluded revision IDs and provider_execution_count=0 in the signed snapshot. Re-run exclusion immediately before signing.

    Acceptance tests

    • Empty existing table passes and reports the exact sorted scope.
    • Missing file/table does not mutate and is unavailable.
    • A parent row, any video narration-child row, or a row inserted between authority read and final exclusion blocks zero.
    • Rows for a different asset or historical parent do not poison the exact claim.
    • Audio does not claim coverage of an invented child namespace.

    Files

    New read-only exclusion helper and focused tests. Do not modify provider execution, claim, hold, reservation, or reconciliation schemas.

    Five-values rigor

    The finite revision set is explicit, reproducible, and limitation-bearing; broad historical queries and narrow parent-only video queries are both rejected.

    Out of scope

    No mixed allocation, settled-cost calculation, provider invoice claim, arbitrary descendant discovery, or write transaction.

    Harness hint

    Use separate reviewers for false-zero scope holes and false-positive historical poisoning.
