Cycle 70: Historical Evidence Collection Reader

Open a durable evidence collection as an exact historical HTML reading, inspect its ordered passages in place, and retain explicit control over research spend.

Spec lineage

Parent: Cycle 69 durable evidence collections.

    Outcome

    An authenticated owner can open /read/derived/evidence/:collectionId. The server-verified collection determines the asset and immutable revision. The reader renders that exact canonical HTML, presents all saved passages in operator order, and navigates to index-derived section anchors. Opening, inspecting, and navigating emit no research event and invoke no model or provider.

    Authority

    Owner scope

    The URL contributes only a collection identifier. Authentication determines owner scope; foreign and absent collections remain the same opaque failure.

    Exact revision

    The verified collection supplies asset ID, revision ID, content digest, and generation. The exact reading response must match every field before any HTML is rendered.

    Anchor projection

    Section anchor and path are read-time projections from the complete immutable revision index. They are never accepted from create or launch clients and do not enter the collection digest.

    Explicit spend

    The collection rail is inspection and navigation only. Research still requires opening ChaseThread and a second explicit confirmation.

    API contract

    • Detailed collection read returns ordered exact sources plus a same-length ordered locations array containing citation ID, chunk ordinal, section anchor, and section path.
    • Read revalidates revision chain, complete index receipt, every source, collection digest, and one-to-one source/location identity in one snapshot.
    • Anchor values come from the verified chunk rows already used by the citation verifier; stored collection rows cannot override them.
    • No new mutation endpoint, redirect endpoint, token, source input, or public share capability is introduced.

    Reader protocol

    1. Validate the collection ID shape before network work.
    2. GET the owner-scoped collection detail.
    3. GET the exact reading named by the verified collection.
    4. Reject unless asset, revision, digest, generation, exact path, source count, and location identities agree.
    5. Render canonical HTML and an evidence rail labeled with historical/current state and ordered passages.
    6. Passage activation resolves an element by exact anchor inside the article, scrolls it into view, and applies temporary emphasis without mutating canonical HTML.

    Race discipline

    • Collection and reading requests share one navigation generation. Route changes and unmount invalidate both.
    • A stale collection or reading response cannot render HTML, replace the evidence rail, scroll, open ChaseThread, or clear a newer error.
    • Repeated passage activation is local and deterministic. Missing anchors report an honest non-destructive error and retain the collection.

    Workflow

    • Saved collection summaries expose an Open command whose target is the collection permalink.
    • The permalink reader shows ordered passages before any research panel is opened.
    • Research collection opens ChaseThread with the already verified collection ID, ETag, and display provenance; launch remains the explicit second gesture.
    • A historical collection visibly offers a separate link to the current revision without replacing the historical evidence.

    Acceptance harness

    • Current and historical collections open the exact revision and preserve ordered locations.
    • Foreign, absent, malformed, tampered, mixed, stale-index, wrong-path, wrong-generation, and source/location mismatch cases render no HTML.
    • Out-of-order collection/reading responses cannot contaminate a newer route.
    • Opening and passage navigation emit zero investigation events; research panel open also emits zero until confirmation.
    • Focused repository/API/React tests, strict typing, production build, diff check, HardenX, and hostile review pass.

    Rejected alternatives

    • Redirect from an unverified URL query to an asset/revision: exposes client-selected provenance.
    • Persist section anchors in collection members: duplicates derived index truth and creates a second drift surface.
    • Find excerpts by DOM text search: ambiguous under repeated prose and detached from indexed citation identity.
    • Automatically open or launch research: converts reading organization into an implicit spend gesture.
    • Public share links: require a separate revocable capability and rights policy.

    Out of scope

    Public sharing, cross-asset manifests, collection mutation/deletion, arbitrary DOM ranges, current-revision migration, and canonical HTML mutation.

    Five-values rigor

    Honesty: index rows locate evidence, not excerpt search. Fairness: owner scope precedes existence disclosure. Rigor: collection and reading identities must agree completely. Diligence: stale requests cannot update the reader. Defensibility: inspection remains free and research remains explicit.

    Harness hint

    Project locations from the verified revision index, add the collection permalink route, share one request generation across collection and exact reading loads, and prove no-spend ordered navigation.
