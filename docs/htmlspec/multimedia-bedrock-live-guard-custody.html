Cycle 34 / Cycle 35

Bedrock live guard custody

Bind current AWS Organizations evidence to an independently authenticated custody and audit attestation without pretending that AWS can make its management account unrevocable.

Status: executableQualification onlyProduction refused

Spec lineage

Cycle 34 accepts an abstract independently authenticated namespace lease. This child makes the operational trust boundary explicit: current Organizations policy evidence, attachment scope, external custody, trusted time, and audit freshness are distinct facts.

  • A future child may add credentialless live evidence adapters and renewal orchestration only after account topology, external signer custody, and audit delivery are deployed and independently tested.

Verdict

AWS Organizations cannot prove prospective unrevocability against its own management authority. SCPs do not constrain management-account principals, and authorized management or delegated administrators can update or detach policies. Cycle 35 therefore proves only a current canonical guard snapshot plus an external custody/audit attestation. It must record the management account as an explicit trust root and keep production eligibility false.

Threat model

  • Detect substitution of organization, management account, workload member account, exact account target, either required policy ID/ARN/type/content, attachment evidence, Cycle 34 lease, custody issuer, trust root, quorum, interval, or audit watermark.
  • Reject self-issued custody, stale or future audit watermarks, audit gaps, mutable policy claims without complete canonical source responses, and renewal gaps.
  • Keep management-account compromise, service-linked-role exclusions, and AWS control-plane compromise outside the proved boundary, while naming them rather than silently treating them as impossible.
  • Do not infer historical lifecycle drainage from CloudTrail absence. Cycle 34's independently authenticated lifecycle-history claim remains a separate prerequisite.

Truthful receipt

The canonical receipt binds all input digests and records current_guard_observed=true, attachment_scope_verified=true, external_custody_authenticated=true, and audit_complete_through_watermark=true. It also fixes management_account_constrained=false, prospective_unrevocability=false, production_eligible=false, bedrock_version_selected=false, and bedrock_read_observed=false.

Protocol

  1. Read the trusted clock and reject caller-time disagreement.
  2. Snapshot verifier identity and authenticate the custody attestation before accepting any caller claim.
  3. Parse every artifact as exact canonical JSON with duplicate-key rejection, strict field sets, exact booleans, lowercase SHA-256 digests, and whole-second UTC timestamps.
  4. Bind the attestation to the exact Cycle 34 receipt and guard snapshot; require configured external signers, plural administration domains, quorum, and audit source.
  5. Bind the Cycle 34 owner digest to the member account; validate both policy matrices, exact account attachments, topology-derived ARNs, and complete raw source responses.
  6. Require the audit watermark at or before observation, within the configured lag, monotonic relative to any predecessor, and backed by the configured independently administered audit source.
  7. Reverify the Cycle 34 qualification after guard checks, then read trusted time again and recheck interval and audit freshness.
  8. Emit only the negative-capability custody receipt. Add no dispatch, production qualification, exhaustion, settlement, or release authority.

Acceptance

  1. Exact evidence deterministically yields one canonical receipt; every field substitution changes identity or fails.
  2. Wrong organization/account/target, missing or extra attachment, wrong policy type, conditional/partial deny, malformed policy, source-response digest conflict, or Cycle 34 digest conflict fails.
  3. Unsigned, self-issued, wrong-root, insufficient-quorum, not-yet-valid, expired, short, overlong, or renewal-gap custody fails before a positive receipt.
  4. Future, stale, regressed, missing, ambiguous, or caller-asserted audit watermark fails. Audit absence beyond the watermark is never promoted to proof.
  5. Caller clock drift or expiry/audit staleness during verification fails after final recheck.
  6. All positive and negative receipt booleans are exact types and immutable under canonical round-trip parsing.
  7. No AWS client factory, credentials, signer, KMS/HSM integration, CloudTrail consumer, policy mutation, route, scheduler, worker, event, graph write, UI, production eligibility, exhaustion, settlement, or release is added.

Five Values

  • Truth: current observation, retrospective audit, and future persistence are separate claims.
  • Care: every authority and trust root is named, bounded, and revocable only outside the proved model.
  • Agency: adapters, verifiers, clocks, and predecessor state are injected; no ambient credentials or hidden singleton decides.
  • Craft: canonical schemas, exact topology, monotonic watermarks, double time checks, and adversarial tests make behavior hard to vary.
  • Restraint: the receipt refuses production and Bedrock-read claims even when every current check passes.

Rejected alternatives

  • SCP equals immutable lease: rejected because SCPs do not constrain management-account principals and can be updated or detached by authorized organization administrators.
  • CloudTrail silence equals no mutation: rejected because delivery and completeness are watermark-bounded and retrospective.
  • One long-lived signature: rejected because it hides renewal, revocation, signer compromise, and evidence freshness.
  • Self-host the signer beside Antiek: rejected because workload administrators could mint their own custody proof.
  • Wire production now: rejected until live account topology, custody, audit, trusted time, renewal, and recovery are deployed and independently exercised.

Official evidence

  • AWS Organizations SCPs: management-account principals are not constrained by SCPs; service-linked roles and outside principals have additional boundaries.
  • UpdatePolicy: management or delegated administrator authority can replace policy content.
  • Organizations service authorization: AttachPolicy, DetachPolicy, UpdatePolicy, and policy-type controls are distinct actions.
  • S3 expiration: lifecycle queues asynchronous delete-marker creation and bucket policy cannot prevent it.

Out of scope

No live AWS or audit credentials, infrastructure deployment, signer implementation, key ceremony, organization mutation, lifecycle-history oracle, production route, job dispatch, billing closure, spend exhaustion, settlement, release, event emission, graph mutation, or UI.
