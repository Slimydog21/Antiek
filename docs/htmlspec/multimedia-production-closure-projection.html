MSR-02D: Immutable Production Projection

Parent MSR-02

Spec lineage

Leaf sprint under MSR-02.

    Contract

    At one UTC cutoff, validate every visual and narration execution through the existing provider execution, signed claim, settled hold, and exhausted reservation authorities. Freeze a separately keyed projection with exact owner digest, asset, revision, production receipt digest, reviewed set ID, narration run ID, role bindings, execution evidence, integer-cent total, basis label, evidence ID, and HMAC.

    Schema rules

    • Basis is exactly production_byte_contributing_settled_provider_executions.
    • Each constituent includes role, scene or chapter ID, execution revision, execution ID, authorization ID, provider/model/capability, charged cents, and settled time.
    • Rows are deterministic and unique. Total is the checked sum. The projection references but does not absorb or rename the separate parent-revision spend snapshot.

    Refusals

    • Any execution MAC, signature, succeeded status, settlement, hold, reservation, cutoff, owner, asset, revision, role, capability, uniqueness, or total conflict.
    • No rows is not zero. A video with only sourced visuals still requires its narration constituents. Local-only production belongs to MSR-03.

    Integration

    First expose the projection as a tested substrate API. Hardening may consume it only after the report schema can preserve both labels and legacy reports without conflation.

    Out of scope

    No mutation, provider invoice claim, refund accounting, budget authorization, route choice, quality optimization, rights decision, or publication.

    Five-values rigor

    The projection is independently verifiable and deterministic; every field either binds an authority boundary or is removed.

    Harness hint

    Use one builder and at least two independent refuters over integrity, cardinality, and accounting surfaces.
