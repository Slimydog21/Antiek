Substrate Atlas

Reframe canonical /stats as a truthful cartographic instrument for Antiek’s durable substrate without inventing graph relationships.

Spec lineage

Root bounded spec; no child specs. Source: operator branding/UI north star plus live audit of Stats/index.tsx at da6d694ac.

    Five values

    • Intellectual honesty: absent count keys render “unknown”, never numeric zero; the image contains no data.
    • Fairness: all table families retain equal information density; visual weight cannot imply one domain is healthier.
    • Rigor: route and WorkspaceWindow heights, refresh, stale-request suppression, loading, error, warning, empty/missing, long counts, and narrow widths receive direct proof.
    • Diligence: sanitize transport and backend warning text at the display boundary while retaining a bounded integrity notice and retry.
    • Defensibility: every metric label and value comes from the frozen table roster plus the current response; no inferred edges, health score, or trend.

    Visual contract

    A quiet polar survey chamber maps blank ice shelves through brass sighting instruments and cyan contour light. Edge-weighted detail preserves a broad central console. Raster is alt="", aria-hidden, non-draggable, pointer-inert, and strongly veiled. Live HTML owns every label, count, state, and action.
