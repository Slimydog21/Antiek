Authoritative live-note presentation

The research workstation must render the durable winner of living-note refinement, never a losing or historically ambiguous attempt.

Identity and authority

  • note.emerged.note_id is the visible note identity.
  • note.refined.note_id is graph-node identity; origin_note_id attaches the outcome to its emerged note.
  • A complete applied outcome contributes new_text; a complete superseded outcome contributes authoritative previous_text, never the losing attempt.
  • The refinement graph node must agree with the visible note's known graph identity; the first complete outcome may establish a missing identity.
  • Only a row created by note.emerged is refinement-eligible; questions and user-authored marginalia cannot be targeted through origin substitution.
  • Legacy null outcomes, partial/impossible authority, identity substitutions, broken chains, and unattached outcomes remain audit evidence but do not become visible truth.
  • After the first authoritative rebase, previous_sequence must equal the row's settled sequence, making reconnect and reordered delivery safe.
  • Visible row identity is first-write-only across emerged notes, questions, and user marginalia; later cross-kind collisions cannot replace a row.

Acceptance

  • An applied outcome updates one existing note through origin_note_id and increments its visible refinement count once.
  • A superseded attempt can rebase stale legacy text to its authoritative prior text, but cannot expose losing text or increment the count.
  • Duplicate, stale, malformed, legacy, and unknown-origin outcomes cannot create or mutate a row.
  • The latest accepted prior text remains available through the existing change disclosure.
  • No backend mutation, provider call, challenge dispatch, graph write, event schema change, or spend authority is added.
