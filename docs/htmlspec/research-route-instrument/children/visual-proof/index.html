Cycle 593 · visual product child spec

The route choice should read like a field instrument, not a settings form.

Research starts with one consequential choice: how much upstream reasoning to authorize. Isolate that choice so its keyboard behavior, truthful budget provenance, unavailable projection, recovery state, and visual hierarchy can be inspected directly rather than only through a monolithic home page.

Spec lineage

Parent: Research Route Instrument

    Design thesis

    Signature: the lens rail.

    Fast and Deep remain two adjacent readings of the same question. The selected lens receives a left-edge sun registration mark and a restrained engraved-policy line; readiness is an instrument status, not a decorative badge.

    Quiet support.

    The budget ledger sits below as calibration evidence. Geometry appears only when authority exists. Details remain subordinate and expandable. No mascot, landscape, generated texture, gradient, or ornamental illustration competes with the decision.

    The deliberate risk is asymmetric selection—not another filled card. A narrow registration rail makes the active route legible at a glance while preserving Antiek’s hard-border editorial vocabulary.

    Rejected alternatives

    AlternativeWhy rejected
    Story the whole StartResearch pageIts network-hook and launch orchestration make state setup brittle and hide the decision surface among unrelated composer behavior.
    Copy markup into a story-only mockA visual snapshot of non-production markup can stay green while the real instrument regresses.
    Add ChatGPT Image artworkThis control’s identity comes from functional registration, typography, and truthful geometry. New pixels would add no authority and would compete with the existing Research Observatory atmosphere.
    Invent a model recommendationNo measured route-quality or trajectory-cost delta exists; selection remains operator-owned.
    Move route state into the componentThat would blur presentation with launch-proof construction and complicate the legacy fallback boundary.

    Acceptance gates

    1. Production StartResearch renders the extracted component with unchanged launch request semantics.
    2. Roving radio behavior skips unavailable routes and retains Home/End/arrow/click behavior.
    3. All four budget authority states have DOM tests and production-component Storybook stories.
    4. LostPixel captures desktop, narrow, and dark proofs without a story-only copy of production markup.
    5. The component introduces no provider/model request authority, budget mutation, cost inference, or synthesis routing.
    6. Focused Vitest, TypeScript, production and Storybook builds, axe, visual review, two heterogeneous critics, and HardenX pass.

    Five-values rigor

    HonestyEvery story must use a response state the API can actually produce; no fabricated projection or “recommended” route.
    FairnessKnown partial evidence remains visible in both one-authority states instead of treating missing cap or spend as zero.
    RigorThe proof matrix covers semantics and geometry; snapshots alone cannot substitute for keyboard and DOM assertions.
    DiligencePreserve StartResearch launch construction and reuse repository Storybook/LostPixel conventions before adding helpers.
    DefensibilityThe extraction must reduce the page’s coupled responsibility and make production states reviewable; otherwise revert it as refactor theater.

    Harness hint

    One file-coupled implementation lane. Refute semantic drift, story-only divergence, keyboard regressions, generic card styling, and unauthorized meter geometry. Minimum two sharpen rounds.
