Werner playback-start beat

Cold-executable contract for the shared ReadAloud surface.

Product truth

  • Emit exactly one voice_playback_started experience only after the first HTMLMediaElement.play() promise resolves.
  • Loading, withheld content, empty text, synthesis failure, rejected playback, stale work, and unmount emit nothing.
  • Pause and resume reuse the audio and do not emit a second start beat; Stop or unmount wins over a pending resume.
  • Choreography is observational: observer failure cannot pause audio or rewrite playback state.
  • Map the event to Werner's existing authored thinking pose; do not add a semantically duplicate bitmap.

Integration boundary

ReadAloud is the sole shared narration owner. It calls an injectable observer after first-play success. The default observer emits through the Werner reaction bus. The component has no data-werner-target, preventing click choreography duplication.

Accessibility and motion

The reaction bus preserves the existing reduced-motion still-pose path. Audio controls, labels, focus order, and status/error semantics remain unchanged.

Acceptance evidence

  1. Reaction-map and signal-helper tests prove the exact semantic event.
  2. ReadAloud tests prove first-play success once, resume deduplication, rejected-play silence, unmount silence, and observer isolation.
  3. Focused tests, typecheck, production build, heterogeneous refutation, hardenx, and exact-head CI pass.
