Evidence-bound canonical twin promotion authority

Turn one structured advisory twin proposal into an owner-reviewed candidate without granting graph mutation authority.

Candidate origin

  1. Only a unique exact Proposed insight: or Proposed question: entry in the verified canonical body is eligible.
  2. Withheld synthesis, arbitrary labels, copied metadata, twin text, and malformed or duplicate proposals are not promotable.
  3. Identity binds owner, source asset and hash, lifecycle binding, publication body and completion, twin document and chunk, kind, text, and ordered evidence.

Evidence

Every candidate has one to sixteen bounded codepoint excerpts. Each binds a non-twin document and chunk, exact range, excerpt bytes, complete chunk hash, and rights class. Evidence must be owner-private or currently servable. Stage, review, and accepted-authority reads re-resolve the canonical publication and every excerpt.

Review

The owner explicitly accepts or rejects the exact candidate digest with a bounded rationale through an Ed25519-signed, nonce-bearing authorization valid for at most fifteen minutes. Each immutable ledger durably pins one exact owner and review key. Candidate and review rows are immutable and replay-exact; append-only events retain canonical payloads and are hash chained. Rejection is terminal. A current accepted read requires full event-chain and projection integrity, matching staging and review events, and current publication, evidence, visibility, and takedown state while the caller uses a coordinator-issued graph connection whose lock remains held. Acceptance is only owner_reviewed_evidence_bound_candidate_v1 for a later canonical graph writer, which must revalidate again in its mutation transaction.

Non-goals

No graph node, edge, finding, gap, citation, embedding, provider call, HTTP route, UI, public sharing, automatic grounding, production mutation, merge, deployment, or spend.

Acceptance

Tests prove exact claim and question staging, owner review and replay, synthesis and label refusal, self-citation refusal, foreign-private evidence refusal, excerpt drift and deletion refusal, candidate digest binding, terminal rejection, immutable schema and event integrity, no graph mutation, Ruff, HTML parsing, HardenX, and hostile review.
