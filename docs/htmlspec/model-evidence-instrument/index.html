Proof before model authority.

Replace Antiek’s invented static quality numbers with a truthful model-evidence instrument. Readiness and price remain operational facts. Only a current, comparable Antiek-bench cohort may produce a measured pick.

       │                         │

missing / partial / stale evidence ───────────────────► NO MEASURED PICK
  • Delete the static task-tier affinity from the quality path; never substitute a number for missing evidence.
  • Rename eligibility as operational eligibility: ready and known within budget. It does not imply quality.
  • A recommendation requires at least two operationally eligible measurements from the same valid report snapshot and task.
  • Do not create a router, inventory, pricing system, benchmark runner, or add-model flow; those are adjacent PR lanes.

Acceptance gates

  1. No _TASK_TIER_AFFINITY, static_prior, or fabricated numeric quality remains.
  2. No benchmark and partial benchmark states produce no measured pick.
  3. Two comparable eligible measurements may select the higher score; unavailable, over-budget, and unknown-budget routes cannot win.
  4. Production component stories cover complete, partial, unavailable, budget-unknown, provider-unavailable, narrow, and dark states.
  5. Focused backend/frontend tests, TypeScript, production build, Storybook, accessibility audit, and zero-diff LostPixel pass.
  6. Two heterogeneous reviewers and HardenX report honestly; blocked engines are recorded, not upgraded to consensus.
