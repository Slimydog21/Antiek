Parent context

This page is cold-executable; no other spec is required.

Antiek already has one shared deterministic arcade engine, Paperclip Zombies during a truthful long research wait, and Ice Fishing in the Library. The operator explicitly wants mascot mini-games on different pages. Brainstorm has a genuine zero-question empty state where no research object is selected, launched, or loading.

Build a third cartridge, Fjord Skip, and offer it only inside that true empty state. The game is a voluntary thinking break. It never claims that play creates ideas, improves research, fills a queue, or progresses a request.

Goal and authority boundary

Ship a deterministic aim-and-skip game with an authored ChatGPT Image fjord, code-owned targets and scoring, keyboard/pointer parity, a discrete reduced-motion equivalent, and an explicit Brainstorm host.

OwnsWithholds
Private aim, throw, skip, target, score, round, seeded-current, rendering, and accessible-status state.Parked questions, selection, launches, navigation, analytics, notes, graph, research status, persistence, network, spend, global Werner position, or product reaction events.

Game contract

ModeInput and result
AnimatedPointer or Left/Right chooses one of five aim lanes. Hold pointer/Space charges within a bounded meter; release throws. A deterministic arc skips up to four times across water. Code-rendered rings score 1/3/5; misses remain misses.
Reduced motionLeft/Right chooses the same five lanes. Each pointer click or Space press resolves the exact seeded throw outcome immediately, announces lane, skips, target, and score, and plays no automatic motion.
RoundSix throws, then a terminal score with explicit retry. Escape belongs to the host and exits. Enter starts/retries without also throwing.

Rigor

1 · Intellectual honesty

Play is not ideation

Copy must call Fjord Skip a break and nothing more. Never say a throw generated, sharpened, ranked, or saved a question; the zero-question gate proves only absence, not cognitive benefit.

2 · Fairness

Do not punish motion preferences

The reduced-motion game resolves the same seeded lane/current/target rules and reports the real outcome. It cannot use automatic arcs, fake points, or a lesser scoring table.

3 · Rigor

Mutate every public edge

Tests must fail if pointer press/release, Space, Left, Right, Enter, or Escape ownership disappears; pure determinism alone cannot prove cartridge mappings or host focus cleanup.

4 · Diligence

Reuse the accepted boundaries

Extend ArcadeMount, the exact-dimension backdrop loader pattern, station suspension, and Lemon controls. A second loop, focus manager, image authority, or mascot gesture is a defect.

5 · Defensibility

Keep pixels subordinate

Record the final ChatGPT Image prompt, master/runtime dimensions and crop. A future image replacement must not change targets, outcomes, controls, accessible status, or tests.
