Werner activity system · SPR-06

Product reaction bus

Let Werner notice meaningful work through one closed, transition-only vocabulary while every product surface remains ignorant of mascot implementation.

Implemented on stacked branch3 milestones

Cold context and goal

Werner already has one shell-owned stage and a finite emote vocabulary. Product surfaces must not import the stage, manipulate mascot position, or invent competing animation state.

Add a six-event experience boundary—highlight, deep-research start/complete/error, idle, and failure—then translate it inside the single production mascot stage. Events are notifications, not durable state. Polling producers must emit transitions, not repeated levels.

Technical milestones

1
Close and validate the vocabulary

Map every allowed product experience to one existing emote and reject unknown or malformed runtime events.

Acceptance: exhaustive typed map; runtime allowlist; one listener with deterministic teardown; no caller override or product authority.

Wire real product edges

Emit highlight only when a selection episode opens, start only after a successful launch action, terminal research reactions only from authoritative outcomes, failure from error feedback, and idle only on the active-to-idle pointer edge.

Acceptance: repeated selectionchange and polling levels stay silent; remount/reconnect does not replay history; abandoned launch provenance expires; failed or budget-halted sessions never celebrate; hidden-tab return and reduced motion own no idle loop; steer failure is immediately visible.

Prove single-stage ownership

Exercise public product events against the mounted production mascot and verify listener removal.

Acceptance: highlight becomes curious and completion becomes happy through the real stage; no second mascot, stage, navigation, network, spend, content mutation, game, or image asset.

Five values

Intellectual honesty

An animation acknowledges work; it never claims the research succeeded beyond authoritative session state.

Fairness

Reduced-motion users receive no involuntary idle loop, while meaningful state remains available in product UI.

Rigor

Level-triggered sources are reduced to edges before crossing the bus.

Diligence

Runtime validation protects the stage even from untyped browser dispatches.

Defensibility

A small closed table connects every experience, animation, source, and test.

Out of scope

  • Arcade cartridges/cabinet, research wait host, generated session images, scene hotspots, Flipbook pixel streaming, or a Werner pose replacement.
  • Product navigation, network, spend, content mutation, stage position, cursor-instrument selection, second mascot, merge, or deploy.
Stop condition: if a producer needs access to WernerStage or mascot position, reject that integration and add a semantic experience instead.
