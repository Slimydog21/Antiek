Give Brainstorm its own quiet idea coast

One explicit route-level visual exception replaces Research's observatory behind /brainstorm with an authored fjord while every question, selection, agent, draft, and game remains semantic HTML.

Implemented5 milestonesChatGPT ImageZero runtime spend

Context and decision

Brainstorm correctly remains in the Research workflow for verbs, tabs, routing, and product authority. It now has a distinct fjord identity through Fjord Skip, but the page substrate still inherits the Research observatory. This sprint permits exactly one visual exception at /brainstorm; it does not widen Workflow, create a per-mode atmosphere registry, or reinterpret generated pixels as controls.

Authority rule: the bitmap is a pointer-inert, aria-hidden environment. The existing glass content, empty-state offer, Fjord Skip canvas, focus order, and mutations remain code-owned.

Five-values rigor

Intellectual honestyCall this a Brainstorm visual exception, not a fifth workflow or a Flipbook stream. The image proves atmosphere only.
FairnessSteelman the four-workflow mapping: per-mode worlds can fragment product identity. The exception survives only because Brainstorm already owns a distinct fjord experience and no other Research route changes.
RigorTests must fail if Brainstorm renders both worlds, Research loses its observatory, pixels capture input, or the runtime asset exceeds 128 KiB.
DiligenceInspect mobile and desktop object-cover crops with real glass content and the Fjord Skip offer/game; do not accept a beautiful standalone plate that fails beneath UI.
DefensibilityRecord the exact prompt, source/runtime dimensions and bytes, generator, rejected alternatives, gates, and exact commit in the handoff.

Rejected alternatives

  • Make Brainstorm a fifth workflow: rejected; that changes product taxonomy for a visual need.
  • Build a generic per-mode atmosphere registry: rejected; one exception does not justify a new abstraction or invite atmosphere proliferation.
  • Reuse the Fjord Skip plate: rejected; the game crop has gameplay composition and would collapse page and cartridge identity.
  • Add Werner, controls, documents, or pseudo-text to pixels: rejected; generated art has no semantic authority.
  • Animate or stream the scene: rejected; no product value defeats the current performance, cost, fallback, and accessibility bar.

Out of scope

No workflow taxonomy change, route/action/tab change, Brainstorm mutation, Fjord Skip mechanics, mascot pose or reaction, cursor behavior, hotspot map, Krea/Modal call, video, network, persistence, analytics, dependency, merge, or deploy.
