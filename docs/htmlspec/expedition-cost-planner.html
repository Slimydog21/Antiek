Canonical `/pricing`

Expedition Cost Planner

Turn a false-authority pricing page into a branded, deterministic what-if instrument. It may explain proposed policy and calculate from operator assumptions; it may never impersonate live provider pricing, an account balance, a quote, an invoice, checkout, or payout execution.

Spec lineage

Root bounded implementation spec. Source: live audit of PR #2712 exact b4cfdbfcc. No child spec is warranted because the UI, pure arithmetic, stories, and proof form one tightly coupled route slice.

Authority boundary

May assertInput values the operator can see; deterministic arithmetic; that the displayed 5M allowance and 50%/10% margins are proposed policy examples.
Must labelEvery derived dollar figure as an estimate or illustration based on the visible assumed provider rate.
Must denyNo account, provider catalog, usage ledger, billing system, invoice, checkout, or payout data is read by this route.
Must not promiseAutomatic tier conversion, model availability, encryption guarantees, creator payment, or an active free tier.

Milestones and acceptance

1 · Extract pure planning arithmetic

Export finite non-negative sanitization and a single plan calculator. Clamp invalid values without ever rendering NaN or negative costs. Round only for display.

Acceptance: focused tests cover below, at, and above the 5M public allowance; private 50% and above-allowance public 10% margins; invalid and extreme inputs; additive totals.

2 · Build the authored planner

Use a route-root div, because PanelLayout owns the one route-level main. Provide native labelled number and range inputs, a polite live results region, and visibly separate assumptions, policy examples, and estimate.

Acceptance: no provider names, master-spec prose, automatic conversion, payout execution, checkout, invoice, or due-now language; the persistent no-live-data statement is rendered.

3 · Add the decorative environment

ChatGPT Image raster is alt="", aria-hidden, pointer-inert, and draggable false. HTML owns every semantic and interactive pixel.

Acceptance: production, image-independent fixture, forced-night, and actual 375×667 narrow stories preserve content and avoid horizontal overflow.

4 · Prove the composed route

Focused RTL tests, TypeScript, token/type-scale/token-parity checks, production and Storybook builds, axe on all new states, hardenx, diff check, and heterogeneous hostile review must pass.

Five-values rigor

Intellectual honestyThe 5M, 50%, and 10% values are displayed as proposed policy examples, never as live billing configuration. A user-entered rate is called an assumption everywhere it influences output.
FairnessDo not advertise named providers or imply one model's cost. The planner treats all operator-supplied rates identically and exposes the full arithmetic breakdown.
RigorTests must independently assert numeric expectations, not reproduce exported constants and call that proof. Landmark composition must be tested under an outer shell main.
DiligenceAudit day, forced night, wide, and real narrow viewports. Validate component and document scroll widths rather than relying on screenshots alone.
DefensibilityEvery strong copy claim maps either to visible user input, pure arithmetic, or an explicitly named proposal. Anything else is removed.
