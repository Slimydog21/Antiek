ANT-RHC / architecture execution spec

Research hard spend ceiling

Turn explicit operator approval into an enforceable provider-dispatch authorization invariant without pretending Antiek can prevent external provider misbilling.

Proposed4 sprintsCurrent main: b36acf284Fail closed

Spec lineage

Root specification. Product context: the existing cascade approval UI truthfully exposes an aggregate stop limit; PR #2034 adds dispatch-boundary rechecks but intentionally permits final in-flight overshoot.

  • No child master is needed yet. The four sprint manuals below own disjoint contracts and form one acyclic execution chain.

Decision

Hard-ceiling invariant: before any billable provider request is sent, Antiek atomically reserves a conservative upper-bound amount from the run ceiling. The request cannot be sent if the hold does not fit. A known outcome settles actual cost and releases the remainder; an ambiguous send keeps the full hold until reconciliation.

The first implementation applies to cascade research provider calls only. The ledger contract should reuse the proven transactional semantics in substrate/midnight_oil/budget_ledger.py, but research receives a narrow adapter and its own run identity rather than importing Midnight Oil product policy wholesale.

Product contract

  • Operators choose stop limit when pricing is unknown or a provider cannot supply a defensible maximum.
  • V1 hard mode is USD-only. It requires every reachable paid dispatch to have known USD pricing, a conservative maximum, provider-supported idempotency and reconciliation, and disabled hidden SDK retries. Other currencies remain stop-limit-only until currency-specific minor-unit exponents are implemented.
  • The approval surface shows ceiling, authorized spent, observed provider spend, held, available, projection assumptions, currency, and eligibility.
  • Free/local calls create a stateful zero-cost attempt receipt without a hold. Paid calls with unknown pricing or reconciliation fail closed in hard mode.
  • The guarantee bounds spend Antiek authorizes. Provider misbilling is recorded honestly as a ceiling-breach incident, freezes the run, and is never hidden to preserve the invariant.

Acceptance invariants

  1. authorized_spent_cents + held_cents <= approved_ceiling_cents after every committed transaction.
  2. observed_provider_spend_cents is separately complete. A breach occurs when one operation's verified actual_cents > projected_hold_cents; equivalently after all resolved operations, cumulative observed spend may exceed cumulative authorized spend.
  3. Every paid provider send has exactly one pre-existing durable hold and one durable send marker.
  4. No retry can duplicate a provider send or release an ambiguous hold; hard mode excludes providers without durable idempotency and reconciliation.
  5. Projection provenance pins provider, model, USD rates, token/tool bounds, upward cent rounding, and version.
  6. Unknown price, non-USD billing, overflow, negative/NaN input, stale approval, changed plan, or changed model fails before dispatch.
  7. Concurrent requests cannot over-reserve; the database conditional update is the authority.

Open questions

  • Which paid research providers expose enforceable maximum output/tool-call bounds today? SPR-01 must inventory actual dispatchers before enabling hard mode.
  • Whether sub-cent pricing needs micros internally. Default decision: conservatively round each reservation upward to cents and retain raw pricing provenance.
  • How long ambiguous holds remain operator-visible before manual reconciliation. Default: never auto-release a sent/unknown hold.
