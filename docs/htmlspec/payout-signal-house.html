Payout Signal House

Status: execution contract for /payouts.

Truth contract

  • The surface is read-only and shows at most 500 newest matching substrate rows.
  • Transferred contributes to provider-accepted totals only when a nonblank provider identifier is present; even then it does not prove settlement, recipient receipt, or external reconciliation.
  • Amounts on escrow, platform, failed, and pending rows are recorded decision amounts, not paid totals.
  • Summary values describe the returned query window, not all payout history.
  • The current endpoint catches database exceptions and returns an empty list, so an empty response cannot prove the ledger is healthy.
  • Raw provider exception notes are not rendered because they are untyped and may contain sensitive transport detail.

Interaction contract

  • Filters apply explicitly, with an exact recipient match and encoded query parameters.
  • Loading never becomes zero; malformed responses enter a safe unavailable state.
  • Stale responses cannot overwrite newer queries.
  • Unknown future statuses and transferred rows without provider identifiers render as unverified without inheriting accepted semantics.
  • Invalid times and unbounded identifiers degrade to bounded labels.

Product gap

A future substrate sprint should stop collapsing read failures to empty, expose a typed availability envelope and cursor/page metadata, and replace raw provider exception notes with typed failure codes plus protected diagnostics references.

Visual and verification contract

ChatGPT Image provides a decorative Antarctic signal house; controls and audit claims remain responsive semantic HTML.
