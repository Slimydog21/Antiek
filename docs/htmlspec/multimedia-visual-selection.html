Reviewed visuals become renderer inputs

Close the storyboard-to-render trust boundary without inventing rights, provenance, or provider authority.

Stack: PR #758Local only

Decision

The timeline owns scene order, disclosure labels, and source chunks. Caller-supplied identifiers are claims, never proof. A mandatory trusted adapter resolves the referenced receipt or rights record and returns a verdict bound to the exact scene, label, and copied content digest. Only then may the compiler seal a private renderer packet.

Acceptance

  • Every bounded timeline scene is covered exactly once and in order.
  • Labels and chunks cannot drift from timeline authority.
  • Every authority verdict binds scene, label, and copied content digest.
  • Inputs are regular, non-symlinked, bounded, digest-matched, privately staged, and atomically published under a process lock.
  • Exact replay returns the verified packet; abandoned staging does not wedge the revision; conflicting or corrupt finals fail closed.
  • Renderer inputs require packet-key and file-digest verification.
  • No network, provider, spend, route, UI, license inference, or publication authority is added.

Rejected alternatives

Passing paths directly to FFmpeg leaves provenance and rights as comments. Treating receipt IDs as proof enables invented authority. Treating a Krea URL as render-ready bypasses quarantine. Inferring a license from metadata converts uncertainty into false authority.

Verification

pytest tests/test_multimedia_visual_selection.py, complete multimedia compatibility tests, strict mypy, Ruff, diff-check, hardenx, and an independent adversarial critic.
