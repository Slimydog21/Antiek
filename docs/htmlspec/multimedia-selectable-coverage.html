Select and combine generated learning arcs

Status: executable sub-spec. Parent: Multimedia SPR-02 Curriculum and Storyboard Planner.

Outcome

After reviewing a no-spend multimedia plan, the operator can keep one generated coverage arc, combine several, choose overview, intermediate, or deep treatment, and persist a focused draft. The original draft remains available for comparison.

Contract

  • CreateMultimediaDraftRequest transports depth and ordered selected_arc_ids into the existing deterministic planner.
  • Arc identifiers must be non-empty, unique, and members of the planner's supported arc registry. Invalid input is rejected; it never falls back to default coverage.
  • The persisted plan is the only source for suggestion identity, selected state, evidence count, teaching outcome, and tradeoff copy.
  • A focused draft is a new asset. It does not mutate, overwrite, or imply approval of the reviewed draft, and it makes no provider call.

Interaction

  • Generated arcs use checkboxes because selection is combinable. Depth uses a three-way segmented radio control.
  • The focused-draft command is disabled when no arc is selected, another command is active, or knowledge finalization is mutating.
  • Reopening a persisted asset restores its chosen arcs and depth from the validated plan projection.

Acceptance

  • One and multiple supported arcs survive store persistence and reopen in the submitted order.
  • Duplicate and unknown arc IDs fail validation.
  • The workstation submits the exact checked IDs and selected depth.
  • Malformed persisted plan identity remains fail-closed through the existing projection guard.
  • Focused planning performs no Krea, TTS, render, authorization, budget, graph, twin, publication, merge, or deploy operation.

Harness hint

Refute silent defaults, duplicate IDs, stale selection after asset switches, and accidental provider execution.
