Materialize renderer-ready narration

Stacked on PR #784. Convert exact manifest-backed chapter audio files into one bounded local narration track for documentary production.

Authority

Require complete ordered ChapterAudio and GeneratedFile authority, exact private local paths, matching hashes and millisecond-normalized durations, then normalize with a fixed local FFmpeg contract. FFprobe durations are rounded to three decimals because every upstream timeline field and renderer boundary is millisecond-native; sub-millisecond sample-grid residue is intentionally not represented as semantic timing. Probe output shape and seal source plus output paths, bytes, shape, order, and identity.

Failure model

Missing, extra, duplicate, aliased, public, oversized, tampered, reordered, millisecond-duration-drifted, or process-failed inputs produce no successful artifact.

Non-goals

No TTS generation, network, routing, spend, HTTP route, frontend, campaign mutation, merge, or deploy.
