Give the canonical My Research monitor an authored Chart Room

Correction to the retired-index concept: /investigations intentionally redirects to /my-research. The old InvestigationsIndex remains retired. Brand the real standalone monitor without creating another composer, list, status model, cost authority, or research door.

Five values

  1. Intellectual honesty: the visual frame describes the monitor already present: research lineages, real status, aggregate spend, and replay/workstation entry.
  2. Fairness: working, done, stopped, unavailable, and needs-attention states retain the canonical plain-language mapping.
  3. Rigor: useInvestigationList({limit: 200}), real budget concurrency, lineage reconstruction, launch navigation, and encoded routes are unchanged.
  4. Diligence: ready, loading, empty, needs-attention, and production-art states receive deterministic proof at 768, 1024, and 1280.
  5. Defensibility: generated art is decorative and subordinate to live HTML; the embedded log under the home composer is visually and behaviorally unchanged.

Surface contract

Only standalone MyResearch receives the Chart Room environment and compact authored masthead. It keeps one launch bar whose actions navigate to the canonical root composer. Embedded <MyResearch embedded /> returns the existing log section with no environment, masthead duplication, or launch bar.

Architecture invariants

  • Do not route to or modify InvestigationsIndex; /investigations continues redirecting to /my-research.
  • Do not add a composer or issue a POST from My Research.
  • Do not change API hooks, budget calls, lineage/status semantics, links, auth gates, or suggested-research behavior.
  • The standalone frame uses a div, not a nested route-level main.

Visual contract

The ChatGPT Image polar cartographic room appears as a restrained full-bleed background with a legibility veil and quiet center. It contains no controls, readable labels, user facts, status, cost, or mascot. Live HTML remains the sole source of layout and interaction.

Acceptance

  • At least five deterministic Chart Room stories plus a production-raster proof exist.
  • Fixture baselines cover 768, 1024, and 1280 pixels.
  • Axe, TypeScript, token/type, Storybook, production build, security, and heterogeneous hostile review are green.
