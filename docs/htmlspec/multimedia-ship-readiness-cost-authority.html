Multimedia Ship-Readiness Cost Authority

Replace caller-supplied cost assertions and manifest estimates with immutable, owner-scoped evidence projected from Antiek's existing signed execution and ledger authorities.

Decision

Do not build a second multimedia ledger. Provider execution, settlement, production receipts, local-run receipts, quality telemetry, and rights review remain distinct authorities. Ship hardening consumes a read-only evidence projection and cannot authorize, settle, route, render, or publish.

The v1 projection is generated at the current time and persisted as an immutable snapshot. It does not claim arbitrary historical reconstruction because provider execution rows are updated in place. It does not infer zero cost from missing rows. It does not invent a revision budget from manifest estimates or execution ceilings.

Current contradiction

  • evaluate_multimedia_asset accepts optional floating-point budget_usd and actual_cost_usd.
  • The real run_hardening path supplies neither and can pass from descriptive manifest rows.
  • Signed provider executions, authorization claims, call holds, and reservations already own accounting truth.
  • Local coordinators and production receipts own explicit local completion; absence of provider rows owns nothing.
  • The manual rights/publication gate and the visual quality advisory are intentionally separate.

Global invariants

  1. All authoritative money is integer cents. Manifest floats remain estimates only.
  2. Authenticated owner, asset, and exact revision bind every snapshot and constituent row.
  3. Callers cannot supply execution IDs, totals, rates, settlement states, or local-zero claims.
  4. Every execution is counted once by verified execution ID; contradiction fails the snapshot.
  5. Missing, corrupt, unsettled, outcome-unknown, failed, cancelled, or post-cutoff accounting blocks readiness.
  6. Zero external cost requires a completed MAC-verified local production authority.
  7. A snapshot is immutable evidence generated at one instant, not an arbitrary historical query.
  8. Hardening performs no provider call, reconciliation wake, reservation, settlement, route mutation, production, or publication.
  9. rights_and_publication remains manual. Quality advice remains advisory.
  10. Existing hardening reports without v1 evidence are historical and non-authoritative.

Five-values bar

Intellectual honesty: unsupported production closure is reported as unavailable, never zero. Fairness: descriptive manifest costs remain visible as estimates without being mislabeled as settlement. Rigor: MAC, signature, hold, reservation, owner, revision, timestamp, uniqueness, and total invariants are mechanically tested. Diligence: paid direct, narration-child, selected visual, sourced visual, diagram, local video, and local audible paths are classified. Defensibility: each report names its schema, evidence ID, cutoff, basis, rows, and exact limitation.

Non-goals

No provider-invoice actual-cost claim; current Krea success is ceiling-settled. No aggregate revision-budget authority. No arbitrary past-state reconstruction. No billing dispute/refund workflow. No automatic quality gate. No rights inference. No autonomous publishing.
