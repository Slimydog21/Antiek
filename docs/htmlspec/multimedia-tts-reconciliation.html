TTS Crash Reconciliation

Resolve ambiguous paid sends and stale local seal claims without duplicate provider calls, silent refunds, or unauthenticated state changes.

Authority Layers

State Transitions

ActionPreconditionPostcondition
quarantine_sendSigned operator action; durable sending marker older than threshold.Full hold charged; provider execution and TTS attempt become outcome_unknown; no retry.
recover_unknownSigned operator action plus externally signed provider job and audio digest.Provider job and private raw receipt bind atomically; attempt becomes received; existing full charge remains.
release_sealSigned operator action; attempt is local-only sealing with durable received bytes.Attempt returns to received; provider state and spend are untouched.
No guessed refunds. Unknown sends retain the full conservative charge even after successful recovery. Independent billing reconciliation is a separate authority and may later establish a lower actual amount.

Invariants

  • Recovery receipts are canonical HMAC artifacts with a maximum one-hour lifetime.
  • Wrong operator, action, execution, timestamp, evidence key, provider job, or byte digest fails before mutation.
  • Recovered bytes are private, bounded, hash-bound, and idempotent only when byte-identical.
  • Provider job transition and recovered TTS receipt commit in one transaction.
  • Parent narration runs continue naturally from the recovered received child.
  • No provider send callback is reachable from reconciliation.
