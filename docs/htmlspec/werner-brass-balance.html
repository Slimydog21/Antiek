Werner Brass Balance

On canonical /pricing, turn the pointer into a small brass balance while Werner remains at his fixed station. The instrument adds atmosphere and feedback; it never becomes pricing authority.

Five values

Value Binding decision Evidence
Truth The scale is decorative and performs no arithmetic, quote, billing, or provider lookup. Capability boundary test and story copy.
Operator control All underlying HTML stays selectable and operable; the instrument has no pointer events. CSS assertion and axe audit.
Calm motion Pointer activity levels the beam; idle settles it once. Reduced motion mounts no custom cursor. Deterministic clock tests and reduced-motion story.
Coherence Use the existing station registry and cursor seam. Werner receives no position input. Registry, selector, and source-boundary tests.
Craft Pure HTML/CSS brass geometry uses Antiek tokens and remains legible in day/night surfaces. Storybook atlas and production build.

Acceptance contract

  1. Exact /pricing and its trailing-slash form select brass-balance.
  2. /pricing-extra, /pricing/compare, and other workflows retain their prior selection.
  3. The instrument reads only live pointer, pointer-idle, and tab-hidden signals.
  4. It is aria-hidden, pointer-inert, absent when disabled, and hidden when the tab is hidden.
  5. It cannot import mascot, stage, navigation, network, document-content, or pricing capabilities.
  6. Werner stays in his neutral fixed-station pose; this sprint adds no mascot movement or ambient claim.
  7. The complete atlas exposes five instruments and the accessibility harness names real Storybook IDs.

Rejected alternatives

  • Move Werner toward prices: violates the ratified fixed-station model.
  • Make the scale calculate cost: confuses decoration with the planner's arithmetic authority.
  • Match every /pricing/* route: grants an unreviewed prefix capability to future pages.
  • Raster or streamed-video cursor: increases latency and cost for geometry that HTML/CSS expresses precisely.
  • Animate under reduced motion: removes the operator's native-cursor safety path.

Harness and stop conditions

Run focused selector, registry, component, and source-boundary tests; then the full Werner suite, TypeScript, design-token/type/parity checks, Prettier, Storybook and production builds, the curated axe/overflow audit, security triage, and independent heterogeneous hostile review. Repair real findings and rerun affected gates. Publish only as a stacked PR on the exact Expedition Cost Planner base; do not merge, deploy, or spend.

The inherited full-matrix gate remains binding. If it exposes a serious violation in an immediately preceding stacked surface, a local contrast repair is allowed only with an explicit board ownership expansion and a successful rerun of the whole matrix.
