Federation Policy Airlock

Status: execution contract for /federation.

Product truth

  • The allow-list gates outbound citation eligibility and inbound acceptance.
  • Allow-list membership does not register a partner, prove trusted status, inspect content, grant legal clearance, or execute attribution or payouts.
  • Opt-in and attribution-consent flags govern outbound citations only.
  • A successful write is not claimed until a valid config is read from the PUT response.

Interaction contract

  • Separate current enforced policy from the editable draft.
  • Adding a partner or disabling a safeguard requires a second confirmation seal.
  • Removing partners and strengthening safeguards may save directly.
  • Failed writes preserve the draft and the previously read current policy.
  • Malformed success responses never replace known-good state.
  • Stale asynchronous responses cannot overwrite newer state.

Visual contract

A ChatGPT Image Antarctic transfer airlock provides atmosphere only. Live controls, labels, states, focus order, dialogs, and warnings remain responsive semantic HTML. The image is empty-alt and hidden from assistive technology.

Verification

  • Deterministic stories: strict, active, expanded-risk, relaxed, loading, saving, failure, malformed, long, narrow, night.
  • Component/controller tests cover local validation, truth copy, confirmation, valid loading, malformed responses, failed draft preservation, and valid read-back.
  • Typecheck, production build, Storybook build, axe-core, LostPixel, security review, and merge-bar review must pass.
