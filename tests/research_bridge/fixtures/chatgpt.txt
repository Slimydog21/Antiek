## Deep research: the on-policy RL market for code agents, 2024-2026

The market for on-policy reinforcement-learning training of code-completion agents has fragmented into three identifiable layers since mid-2024. This report synthesises public filings, conference proceedings, and product-launch coverage to map the layer boundaries and identify the incumbents in each.

### Layer 1 — substrate providers

The substrate layer comprises infrastructure firms that host the compute, the trajectory-collection telemetry, and the policy-update primitives. Prime Intellect [1] is the visible incumbent; their `prime rl run` CLI shipped to public beta in October 2024 [2]. Modal Labs offers an adjacent product line under "RL pipelines" [3] though their roadmap statements through Q4 2024 explicitly defer on-policy RL until 2026.

### Layer 2 — verifier ecosystems

The verifier layer determines what counts as a successful trajectory. The dominant open-weight verifier toolkit at present is `verifiers` (the package bundled with Prime Intellect's prime CLI, not the unrelated PyPI name-squat [4]). Coding-task verifiers from Anthropic's research org appeared in three 2024 papers [5][6][7] but have not shipped as a product.

### Layer 3 — applied vendors

Applied vendors integrate substrate + verifiers into specific code-completion experiences. Cursor and Continue.dev are the public-tier examples [8][9]; Sourcegraph's Cody began publishing RL-tuning blog posts in Q3 2024 [10].

### Open questions

- What fraction of the Tier-1 substrate spend is captured by Prime Intellect versus the long tail?
- Does Modal's deferred entry signal a category-defining structural concern, or just a roadmap choice?
- How quickly does verifier-tooling parity arrive between open-weight and Anthropic-internal stacks?
